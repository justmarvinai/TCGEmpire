# TCGEmpire — Decisions (ADR log)

Short records of decisions that are expensive to change. New decisions are appended; a changed decision
gets a new entry that names the one it replaces. Status: **accepted** unless marked otherwise.
The owner approved the plan with answers on 21 Nov 2026 (`/USER_QUESTIONS.md`).

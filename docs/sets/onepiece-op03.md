# One Piece Card Game — OP-03 Pillars of Strength

> - **Release:** Japan 11 February 2023 · English 30 June 2023 (dates on the official Asian and English product pages, as quoted in search results; one retailer guide says September 2023, which contradicts them).
> - **Real set size:** 123 base cards (8 Leader, 45 C, 32 UC, 26 R, 10 SR, 2 SEC = 89 Characters, 23 Events, 3 Stages) + 27 parallels (8 Leader, 10 SR, 2 SEC, 6 R and the manga-art Sogeking) + 4 SP "wanted poster" reprints (Luffy ST01-012, Kid OP01-051, Doflamingo ST03-009, Kaido ST04-003) = **154 printings**. The official page counts "127 card types" (123 + the 4 SP).
> - **Colours:** Red (Ace and the Whitebeard Pirates), Green (the East Blue villains), Blue (Nami's and Usopp's East Blue), Purple (Water 7), Black (CP9 and the Navy) and **Yellow for the first time** (the Big Mom Pirates). Dual Leaders: Arlong Green/Yellow, Charlotte Linlin Black/Yellow.
> - **Booster (EN):** 12 cards per booster · 24 boosters per display (retailer listings). The Japanese pack has 6 cards and the box 24 packs, as for OP-01 and OP-02 (?); a sealed Japanese case is sold as 12 boxes.
> - **Original MSRP:** US$4.19 per English booster (official product page as quoted; no official display price found, 24 × 4.19 ≈ US$100) · Japanese price not found (?).
> - **Booster artwork:** not verified (?); our picks for the game are under "Booster wrappers".
> - **Box topper:** the official card list has no box-topper parallels for OP-03 (OP-02 had six) (?); the four SP wanted posters are the new extra chase instead.
> - **Pull rates:** Bandai publishes none. One community guide estimates a SEC about every 6 Japanese boxes (?); no estimate was found for the manga Sogeking.
> - **In the game:** the booster can copy OP-01 (12 cards: 7 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Rare, 1 hit slot; `SETS.md` §3).

Card data (numbers, names, rarities, colours, cost/power, effect texts) was taken from the official English card database via the `punk-records` JSON mirror (`english/data/569103.json`), so it reflects current errata text. The game uses its own numbering (`#`) and its own seven tiers; real rarities appear only in the `Real #/rarity` column.

Curation notes:

- **Leaders sit in Rare, not Holo,** as in OP-01. Rare holds the 8 Leaders and the six most wanted real Rares (Teach, the Arlong Park Nami, Luffy, Kalifa, Sanji, Pudding).
- **One card per character, as in OP-01:** the Galley-La disguises of Kaku, Kalifa and Rob Lucci (OP03-059, 060, 071) are out because their CP9 versions are in, and only the East Blue Buggy (OP03-032) is in, not the red one (OP03-008). Leader and Character of the same person stay together as usual (Nami, Rob Lucci, Linlin, Katakuri).
- **The SP wanted-poster Luffy is an Ultra** although its number is ST01-012: it is pulled from OP-03 boosters, it is about the third most valuable English OP-03 pull, and Luffy is in the set (Rare 040), so it fits rule 4 of `SETS.md` §2 by character. Our name suffix is "(Steckbrief)", the German word for a wanted poster. The Kid, Doflamingo and Kaido posters stay out because those characters have no other card in the set.
- **Colour blocks:** Red 001–008, Green 009–021, Blue 022–034, Purple 035–041, Black 042–054 (the Black/Yellow Linlin Leader sits at 043, where the real set puts it), Yellow 055–067. Sogeking (Blue) and Katakuri (Yellow) close the main block at 068/069, as OP03-122/123 close the real set.
- **Complete sub-collections kept on purpose:** all five East Blue villain captains (Alvida, Buggy, Kuro, Krieg, Arlong) with Arlong's officers (Hatchan, Kuroobi, Chew), Kuro's Jango and Krieg's Gin and Pearl; Nami's family (Nami, Nojiko, Bell-mère, Genzo); Syrup Village (Usopp, Kaya, Merry, Usopp's Pirate Crew); all seven CP9 agents (Lucci, Kaku, Kalifa, Jabra, Blueno, Kumadori, Fukurou) plus their boss Spandam.
- **Stages left out:** Striker, Galley-La Company and Enies Lobby, for the same reason as in OP-02 (no `stage` kind; the frame would print EVENT).
- **Omitted on purpose** (vanilla or low recognition, duplicates, Stages): Adio and Lim (characters from the video game *One Piece Odyssey*), Curiel, Speed Jil, Namule, the red Buggy, Haruta, Fossa, Blamenco, Sham, Buchi, Momoo, Carne, Patty, Boodle, the Galley-La Kaku, Kalifa and Rob Lucci, Zambai, Tilestone, Peepley Lulu, Minozebra, Minorhinoceros, Corgy, Jerry, Nero, Brannew, Helmeppo, Wanze, Kingbaum, Bobbin the Disposer, Charlotte Opera, Galette, Chiffon and Praline, Streusen, the three Stages and the Events Cross Fire, Fiery Doll, Out-of-the-Bag, Tooth Attack, Deathly Poison Gas Bomb MH5, Usopp's Rubber Band of Doom!!!, Gum-Gum Giant Gavel, Hull Dismantler Slash, Top Knot, Air Door, Soap Sheep, Tempest Kick Sky Slicer, Ikoku Sovereignty, Tropical Torment and Thunder Bolt (54 cards).
- **Unused real parallels** (reserve pool for events or a later wave): SR parallels of Krieg, Paulie and Cracker; R parallels of Fire Fist, Gin, Zeff and Spandam; the SP wanted posters of Kid, Doflamingo and Kaido.

## Grail and Ultra art briefs (hand-drawn, full art)

Each of these is crafted by hand (up to four review rounds, `ART_DIRECTION.md`). Foil shines on the frame and the artwork, never under the text plates; no text is baked into the art.

- **090 Sogeking [manga art]** (Grail, Blue). *Pose:* kneeling on the edge of a rooftop, cape snapping in the wind, golden mask with its long nose in profile, a giant slingshot drawn to the cheek; a flaming shot has just left it. *Scene:* a black-and-white manga page: across a chasm a huge banner on a tower catches fire; speed lines, screentone sky, one inset panel with a close-up of the mask and a drop of sweat under it. *Colours:* the art is strictly black, white and four greys; colour lives only on the frame (gold foil) and the blue name bar.
- **089 Sogeking [alt art]** (Ultra, Blue). *Pose:* one boot on a rooftop ledge, hand on his chest, mouth open in song, cape flapping, slingshot on his back. *Scene:* sunset over the sea, a small island silhouette, sheet-music notes drifting out of the frame. *Colours:* gold mask, crimson cape, blue sky turning orange at the horizon.
- **084 Portgas.D.Ace [Leader, alt art]** (Ultra, Red). *Pose:* standing on his one-man boat, arms crossed, orange hat tipped low over a grin, flames shooting from the stern as an engine. *Scene:* sunset sea; the fire trail splits the water into two glowing walls behind him. *Colours:* oranges and reds against a deep indigo sea, white spray.
- **085 Nami [Leader, alt art]** (Ultra, Blue). *Pose:* sitting on a giant unrolled sea chart that floats like a magic carpet, winking, weather staff raised; a small rain cloud and a rainbow spring from it. *Scene:* bright blue sky, islands far below, a compass rose on the chart. *Colours:* sky blue, orange hair, tangerine accents, white clouds; the rainbow carries the foil.
- **086 Charlotte Katakuri [Leader, alt art]** (Ultra, Yellow). *Pose:* the huge commander seated alone at a long table, eyes closed in bliss, holding a donut; his scarf has slipped down and the lower face stays in deep shadow. *Scene:* candlelit dining hall, towers of donuts, cream-and-pink wallpaper. *Colours:* maroon scarf, cream and yellow pastry, warm candlelight on dark brown.
- **087 Monkey.D.Luffy [SP wanted poster]** (Ultra, Red). *Pose:* on the poster, Luffy's head and shoulders, straw hat on, the widest grin in the set. *Scene:* a weathered, torn wanted poster nailed to a plank wall, one corner curled, a bullet hole; the header and the bounty are abstract pixel blocks, no readable letters. *Colours:* parchment beige and sepia ink, red frame colour, gold foil on the bounty strip.
- **088 Charlotte Katakuri [alt art]** (Ultra, Yellow). *Pose:* feet planted, trident raised, his body erupting into a wave of mochi that forms giant fists around him; eyes glowing red. *Scene:* golden sky over a candy-island skyline. *Colours:* white and cream mochi, maroon scarf, golden-yellow sky.

## Community lore & meme material

- **The name:** the Japanese set is 強大な敵 ("Kyōdai na Teki", "mighty enemies"); the English title is "Pillars of Strength". It mixes the East Blue villains, Water 7 and Enies Lobby, Ace's crew and Big Mom's family.
- **Yellow arrives:** OP-03 is the first booster with Yellow cards, and Yellow plays with Life: looking at Life cards, adding them, trashing the opponent's. Joke hook: "Gelb spielt mit deinem Leben. Wörtlich."
- **The first SP cards are wanted posters:** Luffy, Kid, Doflamingo and Kaido as "WANTED" reprints, which is why the official count says 127 card types. Luffy's poster is quoted at about US$195 (¥24,800 in Japan); Kaido's at about US$56 raw, Kid's at ¥6,200, Doflamingo's at ¥4,800.
- **Nami got banned:** her Leader text reads "When your deck is reduced to 0, you win the game instead of losing"; blue Usopp, Zeff and Bell-mère trash 7 cards of their own deck on every hit to get there. Bandai banned the Leader in an August 2025 notice (from 30 August 2025 per one retailer) and announced promo P-117 Nami as a replacement (?). "Gewinnt, wenn ihr die Karten ausgehen. Bandai fand das nicht lustig."
- **Sogeking is not Usopp. Officially he is:** the SEC reads "Also treat this card's name as [Usopp] according to the rules." "Laut Regelwerk steckt Lysop unter der Maske. Wer hätte das gedacht."
- **A Leader who refuses to fight:** Iceburg's Leader text starts with "This Leader cannot attack." "Bürgermeister. Greift nicht an, unterschreibt nur."
- **Grail price trail:** the manga Sogeking is CardGamer's most valuable English OP-03 card at about US$950; a PriceCharting snapshot shows about US$250 raw; TCGTalk calls it the set's most liquid high-value card (April 2026).
- **Leaders beat the Secret Rares:** the Nami Leader parallel is quoted at US$135 (2023) to US$302 (2026) (?), Katakuri's Leader parallel at about US$107; the SEC parallels trade far lower, about US$15–50 (Katakuri, whose parallel PRB-01 reprinted) and US$7–13 (Sogeking). "Secret Rare, aber nicht geheim genug."
- **The priciest OP-03 numbers are not in boosters:** Charlotte Linlin [Championship 2024] at about US$1,821 and the Marco championship card at about US$627–1,143; a Pudding winner promo from the OP-03 pre-release events at about US$195 (CardGamer).
- **Merry is a butler:** OP03-052 is Kaya's butler, the man who built the Going Merry, not the ship. "Wer das Schiff wollte: Pech, du hast den Butler."
- **Gaimon** spent twenty years stuck in a treasure chest guarding empty chests (manga). "Wohnt seit 20 Jahren in einer Kiste. Mietfrei, immerhin."
- **Spandam** called a Buster Call by pressing the wrong golden snail phone (manga). "Ein falscher Knopf, eine Insel weniger. Büroalltag."
- **Katakuri** eats his donuts alone and in secret (manga). "Der stärkste Mann der Bande, und er versteckt sich zum Naschen."
- **Vergo** walks around with food stuck on his cheek and never notices (manga). "Hat noch was im Gesicht. Sagt ihm keiner."
- **Teach, still loyal:** OP03-012 shows Marshall.D.Teach as an ordinary Whitebeard Pirate, before he killed Thatch for a Devil Fruit (manga). Thatch's own card fits: "+2000 power during this turn. Then, trash this Character at the end of this turn." "Thatch: eine Runde stark, dann tot. Wie im Manga." "Teach: noch loyal. Noch."
- **German names:** in the German manga Krieg is "Don Creek" (Krieg is the German word for war), Zeff is "Rotfuß Jeff", Iceburg "Eisberg" and Jango "Django" (?). "Krieg heißt im Deutschen Creek. Weil Krieg schon vergeben war."
- **Long wait:** English players got the set four and a half months after Japan (11 February → 30 June 2023).

## Open points

- **English booster wrapper artwork** not verified; the official product page could not be fetched and no search result described it.
- **English pack collation** (12 cards per pack from retailer listings; Leader or DON!! slot) unverified; the Japanese pack size (6 cards) and price were not confirmed for OP-03.
- **English release date:** 30 June 2023 per the official page as quoted; one retailer guide says September 2023.
- **No box toppers** is inferred from the official card list (no C/UC parallels) and not confirmed by a product listing.
- **SP line-up:** one language version of a retailer guide names Luffy, Buggy, Rob Lucci and Charlotte Linlin as the wanted posters; the official card data supports Luffy, Kid, Doflamingo and Kaido for OP-03 (the Buggy, Lucci and Linlin SPs came in later sets).
- **Nami ban:** the date (30 August 2025) and the replacement promo P-117 come from secondary sources quoting Bandai's notice (?).
- **Pull rates:** one community estimate only (SEC about every 6 boxes); nothing found for the manga Sogeking or the SPs.
- **All prices are snapshots** from guides and price trackers that contradict each other (Nami Leader parallel US$135–302, manga Sogeking US$250–950).
- **German names** "Puma D. Ace", "Don Creek", "Rotfuß Jeff", "Eisberg" and "Django" are from general knowledge and were not re-verified (the search budget ran out); the names already used in OP-01 are copied from there.
- **Design decisions to confirm:** the SP Luffy as an Ultra (its base card ST01-012 is not in the set); one Grail (candidate for a second: the Nami Leader parallel); the Galley-La duplicates dropped; the three Stages left out until there is a `stage` kind; 90 cards (the maximum).
- **Art pipeline:** `Gelb` (and `Schwarz`) are new One Piece colours in the content. The frame has hues for both (`art/frames/onepiece.ts`), but the kit scenes (`art/kit/scene.ts`) cover only Rot, Grün, Blau and Lila, so Gelb and Schwarz scenes are needed before the batch.
- **Research limits:** page fetches were blocked (official site, PriceCharting, TCGTalk); the web-search budget ran out before the last checks (German names, wrapper art, starter deck dates, Japanese price).

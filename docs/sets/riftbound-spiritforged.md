# Riftbound — Spiritforged (SFD)

> **Publisher / codes:** Riot Games (English-language production and distribution partner: UVS Games) · Set 2 "Spiritforged" = `SFD` (some price trackers write `SPF`) · Champion Decks: Fiora and Rumble.
> **Release:** Mainland China 12 Dec 2025 (Simplified Chinese) · English global launch 13 Feb 2026 (North America, EMEA, LATAM, Oceania; one retailer listed 27 Feb (?)) · English pre-orders opened 12 Jan 2026 (?) · Pre-Rift (pre-release) events the week before launch; one distributor dates the Pre-Rift Event Kit 6 Feb 2026 (?).
> **Real set size:** 221 numbered cards: six domain blocks of 30 (Fury 001–030, Calm 031–060, Mind 061–090, Body 091–120, Chaos 121–150, Order 151–180; each 10 Common, 8 Uncommon, 7 Rare, 5 Epic), 181–206 the 12 Legends (Rare, odd numbers) with their 14 Epic signature cards (Ornn has three), 207–221 fifteen Battlefields (Uncommon) · plus the six basic Runes R01–R06. Showcase: 30 alternate art (`a`: the 24 champion units of the new Legends + 6 Runes), 30 Overnumbered (222–239: 12 champion units and 6 Seal gear cards; 240–251: the 12 Legends) and 12 Signatures (the 12 Overnumbered champion units, marked `*`) · 3 tokens. Database totals: 302 (riftbound.org), 308 (TCGodds). An early report of 40 Overnumbered (Sheep Esports) was later given as 30 everywhere else.
> **Booster:** 14 cards = 7 Common, 3 Uncommon, 2 foil "Rare or better", 1 foil of any rarity, 1 token-or-rune slot · pack MSRP US$4.99 (?) (only implied by the display price) · **Display:** 24 boosters · MSRP US$119.99 (Riot Merch Store). Riot: a display averages more than 6 Epics and more than 2 alternate-art cards; some packs lack a rare, but every display holds the correct total.
> **Pull rates:** Riot published no breakdown. Retailers assume the Origins rates carry over (Overnumbered ≈ 1 per 3 displays, 1 in 10 Overnumbered signed) (?).
> **New mechanics:** Equipment (Equip, Weaponmaster), Gold (gear tokens), Repeat, Quick-Draw. First set whose Overnumbered printings go beyond Legends (champion units and gear), and whose Signatures are champion units instead of Legends.
> **Launch line-up:** Booster, Display, Champion Decks Fiora (Grand Duelist) and Rumble (Mechanized Menace) at US$19.99 each, Pre-Rift Event Kit, accessories.
> **Supply at launch:** English pre-orders on Riot's merch store sold out in under a minute while the site crashed; bots took most of the stock. Riot paused pre-orders and cancelled fraudulent orders (some legitimate buyers were cancelled too), then switched its store to a drawing (lottery) from Spiritforged on. Booster market price ≈ US$8.56 (TCGplayer, date (?)).

**Naming.** As in Origins, a Legend prints only its title with the champion as a tag; databases write "Irelia, Blade Dancer", which is the form used here. Riot calls the `a` cards "alternate art"; the `222+` cards are "Overnumbered", and a Signature is the same artwork as the Overnumbered card plus the artist's printed signature (CardGamer). All names are the official English names. No German card print could be confirmed (?), so no German names are given.

**Why two Grails.** Ahri, Inquisitive (Signature) is the most expensive Spiritforged card on every tracker found: US$3,700 (Chase Society, 18 Nov 2026), US$3,451 (TCGIndex), about US$2,740–2,752 (TCG Stacked, TCG Watchtower), US$2,664 (TCGodds snapshot, 14 Jun 2026). But Ahri is an Origins champion, and the Origins Grail is already an Ahri. The clear number two, Irelia, Fervent (Signature), is the set's own face (Irelia is a Spiritforged Legend): US$1,710 (Chase Society), US$1,648 (TCG Stacked), US$1,360–1,572 (TCGIndex, two dates). Both are Grails; Ahri gets the higher weight (d 10 vs 9).

**Ultra picks.** The next six Signatures by price: Vayne, Hunter (about US$975–1,326), Teemo, Strategist (about US$531–1,100), Soraka, Wanderer (about US$700–1,000), Yone, Blademaster (about US$603–730), Bard, Mercurial (about US$395–636) and Yasuo, Windrider (about US$505). Left out: Darius, Executioner (about US$382, TCGodds Jun 2026), Sett, Brawler, Aphelios, Exalted and Karma, Channeler (prices not found, lower). Every Ultra and Grail is a champion unit, not a Legend: that is how this set's Signatures work.

**Tier rule deviations.**

- Ahri, Inquisitive is an Origins card. Inside this set it exists only as the unsigned Overnumbered (Alternative, ~US$662 per Chase Society) and the signed Grail: a deliberate near-miss, as in the real packs. Vayne, Teemo and Yasuo (Ultra) likewise have no base card in the Spiritforged main set.
- Two Overnumbered cards sit in Alternative (the real showcase printings with the highest prices below the Signatures); Origins used only `a` cards there.
- Bard, Mercurial and Soraka, Wanderer (real Rares, bases of two Ultras) sit in Uncommon, like Origins' side champions; Yone, Blademaster (real Epic) is in Rare.
- Epic champion units in normal art would be Holo by the brief; as in Origins they stay in Rare so that Holo holds exactly the 12 Legends.

## Grail and Ultra art briefs (hand-made, full art)

All eight are borderless full-art heroes in a night spirit-festival look (our own take on the set's Spirit Blossom theme (?)): paper lanterns, drifting blossom petals, soft spirit light. Each carries a small unreadable gold signature scrawl in a lower corner.

- *Ahri, Inquisitive (Signature)* (Grail, Mind): the fox spirit sits on the curved ridge of a shrine roof, leaning forward with her head tilted and a curious half-smile, one hand holding up a glowing orb shaped like a peach blossom; nine translucent tails fan out behind her into a halo of petals; a huge pale moon and strings of lanterns below. Rose pink, violet, moon white, warm lantern gold.
- *Irelia, Fervent (Signature)* (Grail, Calm): Irelia balances barefoot on the flat of a single floating blade above a mirror-still lake, eyes closed, arms opening like a dancer; the rest of her blades form a crescent fan behind her like wings, ribbons of petals spiral up; floating lanterns on the water. Teal, pale pink, silver blades, gold accents.
- *Vayne, Hunter (Signature)* (Ultra, Fury): Vayne crouches on a temple roof ridge, aiming a small crossbow straight down at the viewer, long coat flaring, a bolt of spirit light ready; the moon behind her outlines her shades. Deep crimson, black, moonlit blue, white petals.
- *Bard, Mercurial (Signature)* (Ultra, Mind): the cosmic wanderer floats cross-legged above a lantern festival, ringing a bell-shaped instrument; chimes and three tiny round spirit creatures trail behind him in a looping path of stars. Cream, gold, deep indigo, pink sparkles.
- *Teemo, Strategist (Signature)* (Ultra, Mind): the little scout sits on top of a glowing mushroom lantern, studying a big hand-drawn map on his knees with a pencil behind his ear; tiny spirit flowers and mushrooms glow around him in the grass. Soft green, peach, lantern orange, indigo night.
- *Yone, Blademaster (Signature)* (Ultra, Body): the masked swordsman stands in the middle of a red arched bridge, two swords drawn and crossed, one bright steel, one spectral red; a torii gate and mist behind, petals cut in half mid-air. Blood red, black, ghostly white, pink.
- *Yasuo, Windrider (Signature)* (Ultra, Chaos): Yasuo rides a gust of wind across a field of floating lanterns, katana drawn low, scarf and ponytail streaming; the wind is drawn as teal ribbons full of petals. Teal, white, night blue, gold.
- *Soraka, Wanderer (Signature)* (Ultra, Order): the horned healer walks barefoot through a glowing meadow, crescent-topped staff in hand, small star spirits following her like fireflies; a shooting star overhead. Lavender, mint, silver starlight, gold.

## Booster wrappers

Wrappers reuse the hero artworks (decision 064). Recommended three:

1. **Irelia, Fervent (Signature)** — the set's own face and Grail; Irelia also appears to be on a real Spiritforged wrapper (image names in a Dot Esports article suggest Irelia, Lucian and Ezreal packs (?)).
2. **Yone, Blademaster (Signature)** — the strongest red-and-black silhouette of the set, a fan favourite.
3. **Vayne, Hunter (Signature)** — the top Ultra; crimson against Irelia's teal and Yone's red-black keeps the three packs apart.

Ahri is left off on purpose: the Origins wrapper already shows Ahri, and her Grail stays the surprise. If the owner prefers the top chase on the pack, swap Vayne for Ahri.

## Community lore & meme material

- **Sold out in under a minute, again.** English pre-orders sold out in under a minute, the store crashed and bots got most of the stock (Dot Esports, PCGamesN). Riot paused pre-orders and cancelled fraudulent orders; some legitimate buyers were cancelled too.
- **A lottery for cardboard.** From Spiritforged on, Riot's merch store sells by drawing: enter in a window, and if drawn you get a short window to pay (Riot FAQ). Resellers wrote that bots lost their edge but casual resellers gained one.
- **Packs without a rare.** A packing error from Origins left some boosters without a rare; Riot said it would also hit Set 2 and only be fully fixed with Set 3, while every display still holds the right total (TechRadar, riftwatch).
- **Ahri, again.** The top chase of Spiritforged is not a Spiritforged champion: Ahri, Inquisitive (Signature), an Origins unit reprinted as a Spirit Blossom Overnumbered, at US$2,600–3,700. The Origins Grail is also an Ahri. Even her unsigned version (~US$662) beats every Spiritforged Legend.
- **Signed units, unsigned Legends.** First set whose Signatures are champion units; the twelve new Legends only got unsigned Overnumbered versions (the best, Irelia, ~US$116–180).
- **Draven ban.** In Riftbound's first bans (effective 31 Mar 2026) Riot banned Draven, Vanquisher (SFD-020) and Called Shot (SFD-122), among others, and wrote that it "made a mistake" with Draven's Legend; it banned the unit, not the Legend, so Draven decks stay playable.
- **More bans.** Emperor's Dais (SFD-207) and Stellacorn Herder (SFD-048) were banned from 23 Nov 2026; Riot explained the battlefields as too strong with Riven (they remove the risk of conquering).
- **The item shop in cardboard.** Equipment brings League's shop to the table: Doran's Blade, Shield and Ring, B.F. Sword, Long Sword, Trinity Force, Blade of the Ruined King, Guardian Angel, Rabadon's Deathcrown, Warmog's Armor, plus Gold tokens.
- **Needlessly Large Yordle** is a real card name (SFD-055, Rare).
- **Poro corner.** Lonely Poro, Plundering Poro, Veteran Poro and Poro Snax are in this set; in Unleashed the Lonely Poro got an Overnumbered printing worth about US$231–331.
- **Equipment praised, battlefields "a mixed bag"** (ComicBook's "7 things we loved and hated").
- **Origins champions in Spiritforged packs.** Riftbound.gg noted that some Spiritforged packs contain new Overnumbered champion units of Origins cards ("this beautiful new Ahri").
- **Jax and his lamp post (LoL meme, not re-verified this pass).** Jax famously fights with a street lamp; "Grandmaster at Arms" with no proper weapon.
- **"Shurima! Your emperor has returned!" (LoL meme, not re-verified this pass).** Azir's signature line; fits Emperor's Dais getting banned.

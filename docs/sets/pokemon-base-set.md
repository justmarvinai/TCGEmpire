# Pokémon — Basis-Set (Base Set)

Curated in-game version of the real Pokémon TCG Base Set: **90 cards** (84 real cards + 6 alternate-art cards), mapped onto the game's 7 tiers. "(?)" marks anything that could not be verified.

- **Release:** Japan 20 Oct 1996 (as "Expansion Pack", Media Factory) · USA 9 Jan 1999 (Wizards of the Coast) · Germany December 1999 (exact day not found (?)). The German set is listed as "Grundset" on PokéWiki and as "Basis-Set" on Bisafans and in hobby usage.
- **Real set size:** 102 cards = 16 Holo Rare, 16 Rare, 32 Uncommon, 32 Common, 6 basic Energy (no rarity symbol).
- **Booster and display:** 11 cards per booster (1 Rare, 3 Uncommon, 5 Common, 2 basic Energy; the Rare is a Holo in roughly 1 of 3 packs, a community estimate), 36 boosters per display. Japanese original: 10 cards per pack, 60 packs per box.
- **Booster artwork:** three wrappers, one each with Glurak (Charizard), Turtok (Blastoise) and Bisaflor (Venusaur). The artwork has no effect on contents.
- **Original MSRP:** about US$3.29 per booster (about US$118 per display) in 1999; ¥291 printed on the Japanese pack; German DM price not verified, anecdotally 7–8 DM (?).
- **1. Edition:** the first print run, marked with the "Edition 1" stamp below the left corner of the picture frame; the rarest and most expensive print.
- **Shadowless:** the English print run right after 1st Edition, without the stamp but with the same early layout (no drop shadow on the picture frame, thinner type). It does not exist in German: the German 1. Edition already has the shadow.
- **Unlimited:** the mass print run with drop shadow and bolder type; the cards most people owned. A late "1999–2000" copyright variant was printed for the UK.

Notes on the mapping:

- All 16 real Holo Rares are in the list: 12 as Holo, 3 as Ultra, 1 as Grail.
- Tiers follow the real rarity except for the special tiers. Doppel-Farblos-Energie keeps its real Uncommon rarity (the one energy people are glad to pull); the six basic Energy cards are the bottom of Common. Moving it to Common would give Common 35 / Uncommon 20.
- Alternative cards: Pikachu (Rote Wangen) is based on a real variant. Vulnona (Ohne Schaden) is inspired by a real misprint, but the real card has the normal artwork, so the alternate art is game-original. Arkani, Garados, Simsala and Dragonir are fully game-original alternate arts.
- Omitted real cards (18): Dodu, Traumato, Tangela, Rattfratz, Rattikarl, Jurob, Jugong, Magmar, Rossana; Piepi-Puppe, Rückentwicklungs-Spray, Aufwisch, Defender, Energiezugewinnung, Hyperheiler, Wartung, Pokémon-Flöte, Beleber. First candidates to add back if the list grows: Rattfratz + Rattikarl, Magmar, Rossana, Defender.

## Community lore & meme material

- **Pack weighing:** a holo card adds roughly 0.3 g, so sellers put loose boosters on pocket scales; listings still say "heavy", "light" or "unweighed", and the often-quoted 21 g threshold is disputed.
- **Guaranteed energy:** every real booster held 2 basic Energy cards; energy was the one pull that was always guaranteed.
- **Shadowless:** early English cards lack the drop shadow on the picture frame; these copies are scarcer and sell for clearly more than Unlimited, and mislabelled "Shadowless" listings are common.
- **German 1. Edition:** the German set came out in December 1999 with a stamped 1. Edition run, but there is no German Shadowless; the German 1. Edition has the shadow and differs only by the stamp.
- **Red Cheeks Pikachu:** Wizards recoloured the artist's yellow cheeks to red for early English prints and later reverted it, which made a variant of a common card a chase card.
- **No Damage Ninetales:** some English copies, mostly from Brushfire theme decks, print the attack Fire Blast without its damage number "80".
- **Small-print errors:** early English Voltorb cards say "Monster Ball" instead of "Poké Ball", and Vulpix reads "HP 50" instead of "50 HP".
- **The Machamp trap:** holo Machamp came only in the 2-Player Starter Set, never in boosters, and those copies carry a 1st Edition stamp, so the stamp on a Machamp does not signal a rare card.
- **Prerelease Raichu:** a Raichu stamped "PRERELEASE" was dismissed as a myth until a former Wizards employee showed a photo in 2006; about 8–11 copies are said to exist, and a PSA 6 sold for US$550,000 at Heritage on 19 Sep 2025.
- **School bans:** in autumn 1999 schools across the USA banned the cards (Education Week, Nov 1999); in Germany a primary school in Baden-Württemberg issued the first reported ban in autumn 2000, citing unfair trades by older pupils and lunch money spent on cards (ZDF).
- **"A lottery for kids":** in September 1999 parents filed a class action in San Diego against Nintendo, Wizards of the Coast and 4Kids, claiming booster packs were illegal gambling.
- **Glurak record prices:** a PSA 10 1st Edition Shadowless Charizard sold for US$369,000 (Dec 2020), US$420,000 (PWCC, Mar 2022), US$550,000 (Heritage, Dec 2025) and US$954,800 (Goldin, Feb 2026); reports of about US$1.38 million in Oct 2026 were still unconfirmed (?).
- **PSA 10 pop:** only about 121–125 of more than 5,000 graded 1st Edition Charizards are PSA 10, roughly 2–2.5%.
- **Logan Paul, part 1:** he walked out to his June 2021 fight against Floyd Mayweather wearing a 1st Edition Charizard (bought for about US$150,000, regraded BGS 10) as a necklace.
- **Logan Paul, part 2:** in January 2022 his US$3.5 million "sealed 1st Edition Base Set case" was opened and contained G.I. Joe cards.
- **Sealed and test-print records:** at Heritage in January 2021 a sealed 1st Edition display sold for US$408,000 and a 1998 Wizards "Presentation" test-print Blastoise for US$360,000.
- **Kadabra and Uri Geller:** Geller sued over Kadabra in 2000; no Kadabra card was printed from 2002/2003 until 2023, after he withdrew his objection in late 2020 ("I was a fool").
- **German Glurak:** a German 1. Edition Glurak in PSA 10 was reported sold for about €25,000; one source counts 53 PSA 10 copies (?), far below English prices.
- **Dachbodenfund:** "Dachbodenfund" (attic find) is the stock phrase of German classified ads for old collections; GamePro reported a UK man who found cards in the attic before his wedding and sold three for about €37,741 (Skyridge Gluraks, not Base Set).
- **Haymaker:** a dominant early tournament deck ran 70-HP basics such as Nockchan and Elektek with PlusPower and Windhauch (Gust of Wind) instead of big evolutions like Glurak.

## Open points

- **No direct database access:** pokewiki.de and bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net could not be opened from this session (hosts not on the network allowlist). All facts were checked through web-search results that quote PokéWiki, Bisafans, Bulbapedia, PokeBeach and news sites. A human spot check of the German names against the PokéWiki page "Grundset (TCG)" is recommended.
- **"Windstoß" is not the card name:** the German Gust of Wind is "Windhauch" (93/102) on both PokéWiki and Bisafans; the list uses "Windhauch".
- **German name spellings that vary by database:** "Super Energie-Absauger" (hyphenation), "Pokémon Center" vs. "Pokémon-Center", and "Doppel-Farblos-Energie", which PokéWiki says was formerly "Doppelte farblose Energie" (which wording the 1999 print used is unverified (?)).
- **German set name:** "Grundset" (PokéWiki) vs. "Basis-Set" (Bisafans, this project); which one counts as official was not confirmed (?).
- **German release day and price:** only "December 1999" was found; the DM price per booster is unverified (one forum post says 7–8 DM; a collector document mentions 144 DM, apparently for a whole display (?)).
- **German distributor in 1999:** PokéWiki says Wizards of the Coast distributed the game at first and Amigo took over in 2003; not confirmed by a second source (?).
- **Red Cheeks Pikachu in German:** the variant is confirmed for English prints only; whether German copies exist with red cheeks is unverified (?).
- **No Damage Ninetales print run:** sources disagree on whether the error sits in Shadowless or Unlimited copies.
- **Red Cheeks distribution:** Bulbapedia estimates a 50/50 split in 1st Edition and Shadowless; whether early Unlimited copies have red cheeks is disputed.
- **Holo pull rate:** "1 in 3 packs" is a community estimate; no official figure exists.

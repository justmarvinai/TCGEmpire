# Riftbound — Origins (OGN)

> **Publisher / codes:** Riot Games (English-language production and distribution partner: UVS Games) · Set 1 "Origins" = `OGN` · companion starter set "Proving Grounds" = `OGS`.
> **Release:** Mainland China 1 Aug 2025 (Simplified Chinese; date as announced (?)) · English global launch 31 Oct 2025 (North America, EMEA, LATAM, Oceania; local game stores first).
> **Real set size:** 298 numbered cards (12 Legends, 12 Signature Spells, 6 basic Runes, Battlefields, units/spells/gear in Common / Uncommon / Rare / Epic) + 30 alternate-art "a" cards (24 champion units + 6 runes) + 12 Overnumbered Legends (299–310/298) + 12 Signature Overnumbered (299\*–310\*/298) = 54 collector variants, 352 entries in total.
> **Booster:** 14 cards = 7 Common, 3 Uncommon, 2 foil "Rare or better" (Rare / Epic / Alt Art / Overnumbered), 1 foil of any rarity, 1 token-or-rune slot · MSRP US$4.99. **Display:** 24 boosters · MSRP US$119.99.
> **Pull rates:** Alt Art ≈ 2 per display · Overnumbered ≈ 1 per 3 displays (~1:72 packs) · Signature ≈ 1 in 10 Overnumbered (official) ≈ 1 per 30 displays (~1:720 packs) · one specific Signature ≈ 1:8,640 packs (derived; assumes an even split over 12).
> **Launch line-up:** Booster, Display, 3 Champion Decks (Jinx, Viktor, Lee Sin; US$19.99 each, each with 1 booster), Proving Grounds box (Annie, Master Yi, Lux, Garen; US$29.99), plus a Riot-Store-exclusive Arcane Box Set (shipping was delayed; contents (?)).
> **Organised play:** "Nexus Night" = the casual in-store play night with exclusive promo printings (e.g. a Nexus Night promo of Blazing Scorcher, OGN-001); store tournaments appear as "Origins Skirmish" events in community databases; prize structure and names of larger events (?).
> **Supply at launch:** Riot Store pre-orders (opened 4 Aug 2025 (?)) appeared to sell out within minutes amid checkout crashes; caps per Riot ID were 2 displays / 3 Proving Grounds / 3 of each Champion Deck; Riot later admitted pre-orders shipped far too late; Proving Grounds was reprinted; sealed still trades above MSRP in 2026 (pack ≈ US$9.44, display ≈ US$179). LGS allocation sizes and launch scalper prices: (?).

**Naming.** The printed name of a Legend is only its title ("Loose Cannon"), with the champion as a tag; databases and retailers write "Jinx, Loose Cannon", which is the form used here. Champion units are printed as "Jinx, Demolitionist". Riot calls the `a`-suffix cards "alternate art"; the wiki files alt-art and overnumbered cards under "Showcase" and signed ones under "Signature Showcase". All names below are the official English names. Riot's website and news are localised into German (e.g. "Sammelmöglichkeiten in Riftbound: Origins"), but a German print run of the cards could not be confirmed (?), so no German card names are given.

**Why Ahri is the Grail.** Her Signature is the highest-priced Origins card on every tracker found but one: US$3,711 (12 Nov 2026, TCGplayer market price via Crypto.com's guide), about US$2,900 in Aug 2026 (riftboundguide), about US$2,770 on TCG Watchtower, with completed sales of US$4,200 (6 Aug 2026) and US$3,150 (22 Sep 2026). She already led in Jan 2026 at about US$1,650 (LoLNow). The exception is TCGIndex, which briefly had Kai'Sa ahead (about US$2,755 vs US$2,678).

**Ultra picks.** Kai'Sa Signature is the clear number two (US$2,739–3,114). Miss Fortune follows (US$1,401–1,526), then a near-tie of Jinx (about US$1,070–1,240), Lee Sin (about US$1,000–1,140) and Teemo (about US$1,020); Yasuo trails (about US$770–890). Lee Sin got the fourth slot over Teemo for the Faker tie-in; swap freely.

**Tier rule deviation.** Holo holds exactly the 12 Legends. Epic champion units in normal art would also be Holo under the brief, but adding them would push Holo to 24, so they sit on the reserve bench below the card list.

## Community lore & meme material

- **Sold out in minutes.** Riot Store pre-orders "appeared to sell out within minutes" while checkout crashed; PCGamesN headlined it as "overwhelming demand" causing "pre-order chaos".
- **Anti-scalper caps.** Riot limited pre-orders per Riot ID to 2 displays, 3 Proving Grounds boxes and 3 of each Champion Deck.
- **"Way too long".** Riot's own "Origins Launch Learnings" post conceded that pre-orders took far too long to arrive; some buyers were still waiting after street date.
- **Arcane box delay.** The Riot-Store-exclusive Arcane Box Set shipped late (PCGamesN).
- **Signature maths.** Officially about 1 in 10 Overnumbered cards is signed; with roughly one Overnumbered per three displays that is one Signature per ~30 displays, or "1 in 700+ packs" (CardGamer).
- **No-reprint promise.** Riot stated it will never reprint an Overnumbered card with the same art and frame.
- **Ahri's climb.** Ahri Signature: about US$1,650 (Jan 2026), about US$2,900 (Aug 2026), US$3,711 (12 Nov 2026); single sales reached US$4,200.
- **Jinx is not the top chase.** Despite Arcane and her own Champion Deck, Jinx Signature was about US$720 at launch (Nov 2025) and about US$1,070–1,240 in 2026 — fourth to sixth place.
- **Sealed premium.** A 2026 tracker snapshot shows boosters at about US$9.44 and displays at about US$179, against MSRP US$4.99 / US$119.99 (TCGodds; snapshot date (?)).
- **Starter-box exclusives.** Proving Grounds holds 24 mechanically unique cards; Riot said future starter products will not contain unique cards, and the box had to be reprinted.
- **Eternal rune bulk.** Every booster has a token-or-rune slot, and the six basic Runes return in every later set (SFD, UNL, VEN as R01–R06); the alt-art runes (007a etc., artist Fairfoul) are the consolation prize.
- **Recycled art.** The wiki notes Origins cards reusing Legends of Runeterra art: Noxus Hopeful uses LoR's "Trifarian Hopeful", Heimerdinger, Inventor uses LoR's level-1 Heimerdinger.
- **Ban hammer (?).** The wiki's ruling log lists Ekko, Recurrent as banned in Standard Constructed from 18 Sep 2026 and a Zhonya's Hourglass ban dated 23 Nov 2026; neither was cross-checked.
- **Teemo hate and Yasuo's 0/10 (LoL meme, not re-verified this pass).** Teemo is the community's favourite champion to hate (invisible mushrooms); Yasuo players are said to hit their "power spike" at 0 kills and 10 deaths.
- **"200 years" (LoL meme, not re-verified this pass).** A Riot designer's 2020 (?) remark about "200+ years of collective game design experience" is quoted whenever a new champion kit looks overloaded.
- **Faker (LoL lore, not re-verified this pass).** Faker's 2024 Hall of Legends honour came with Ahri skins whose top bundle cost roughly US$450–500 (?) — a ready-made echo for an Ahri Grail; Lee Sin is the classic "outplay" champion.

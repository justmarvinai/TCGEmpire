# Pokémon — Dschungel (Jungle)

Curated in-game version of the real Pokémon TCG expansion Jungle, the first English expansion after the Base Set: **78 cards** (all 64 real cards + 14 alternate-art cards), mapped onto the game's 7 tiers. "(?)" marks anything that could not be verified.

- **Release:** Japan 5 Mar 1997 as "Pokémon Jungle" (48 cards: 16 Common, 16 Uncommon, 16 Holo Rare) · USA 16 Jun 1999 (Wizards of the Coast) · Germany June 2000 as "Dschungel", about a year after the English set (PokéWiki; exact day not found (?)).
- **Real set size:** 64 cards = 16 Holo Rare (1–16), the same 16 Pokémon again as non-holo Rare with identical artwork (17–32), 16 Uncommon (33–48), 16 Common (49–64: 15 Pokémon and Poké-Ball, the set's only Trainer). No Energy cards. Each block is numbered alphabetically by English name.
- **Booster and display:** 11 cards per booster: 7 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Rare; the Rare is a Holo in about 1 of 3 packs (Pojo, PSA; an estimate, no official figure). 36 boosters per display. Japanese original: 10 cards per pack, sold in a long and a short wrapper variant.
- **Booster artwork:** three English wrappers, one each with Knuddeluff (Wigglytuff), Sichlor (Scyther) and Flamara (Flareon). The Japanese pack shows a group: Menki, Smettbo, Rizeros, Kokowei, Paras, Myrapla and Knofensa. The artwork has no effect on contents.
- **Original price:** the booster MSRP was not found; most likely the same as the Base Set, about US$3.29 (?). The two theme decks cost US$9.99 each (PokeBeach). German DM price not found (?).
- **Print runs:** 1st Edition (stamp) and Unlimited, in English and in German ("1. Edition"). The English 1st Edition reportedly sold out within about two weeks (Bulbapedia, quoted by PSA). All 16 Unlimited holos were also printed without the set symbol by mistake ("No Symbol").
- **Theme decks:** Power Reserve / "Kraftreserve" (Pflanze/Psycho) and Water Blast / "Wasserschwall" (Wasser/Kampf, holo Aquana), released together with the set.

Notes on the mapping:

- Nothing is cut. The real set has only 48 different cards, so every real card is in and every evolution line inside the set stays complete. Lines that start in the Basis-Set continue here (Tauboga → Tauboss, Voltobal → Lektrobal, Safcon → Smettbo, Piepi → Pixi, Ponita → Gallopa); Dodri's basic form Dodu was cut from our Basis-Set.
- All 16 real Holo Rares are in: 9 as Holo, 5 as Ultra, 2 as Grail.
- The Rare tier is the 16 real non-holo Rares, the "twins" of the holos with the same artwork, named "(Non-Holo)" so binder and market can tell them apart. Art: a twin reuses its holo's kit spec without foil; the twins of Ultra and Grail cards get a simple kit version of the hand-made motif. No new drawing.
- Uncommon and Common follow the real rarity exactly. Jungle has no Energy cards, so the set has none and its booster needs no energy slot.
- **Two Grails:** Relaxo and Aquana are far ahead of every other card in all price sources (raw about twice the next holo, 1st Edition PSA 10 about four times) and swap first place between sources, so both get d 10. Relaxo is the card most guides call "the" chase.
- **Five Ultras:** Blitza and Flamara (with Aquana the "Big Three" Eeveelutions), Sichlor and Knuddeluff (wrapper stars, with Flamara) and Tauboss (the next market value). Five instead of six keep 9 cards in the Holo tier, which is drawn 200 times as often as Ultra; the sixth would be Pantimos or Pixi.
- **Alternatives:** three are named after real variants but get game-original art, like Vulnona (Ohne Schaden) in the Basis-Set: Pixi (Prerelease) (real PRERELEASE stamp), Pikachu (W-Stempel) (real promo with a gold W stamp) and Aquana (Ohne Symbol) (real "No Symbol" misprint). The other eleven are game-original alternate arts of the set's fan favourites, including the Grail Relaxo and the Ultras Sichlor, Flamara and Blitza.

## Grail and Ultra art briefs (hand-made, full art)

Drawn by hand (up to four review rounds, `ART_DIRECTION.md`). These are our own compositions, not copies of the real card art. Each card's Non-Holo twin is a kit version of the same motif; its Alt-Art must read as a clearly different picture.

**Grail**

- *Relaxo* (Grail, Farblos, 078). **Pose:** lies on its back across the whole width of the card, belly up like a hill, arms flopped to the sides, mouth open in a snore, one foot twitching; a big "Z" bubble rises. **Scene:** a narrow jungle path disappears under its body; small silhouettes of jungle animals wait on both sides; three butterflies sleep on its belly; dawn mist and light rays through the canopy. **Colours:** deep teal-blue fur, cream belly and face, emerald and moss greens, warm gold light; rainbow-gold full-art frame. **Why Grail:** the card most guides call the Jungle chase (Bill's Archive, Card Codex, The Hobby Bin), and the road block of the original games.
- *Aquana* (Grail, Wasser, 077). **Pose:** three-quarter view on a mossy stone in a jungle lagoon, head raised to the moon, frill collar spread, fin tail curling behind; the tail tip melts into the water. **Scene:** night lagoon with a waterfall behind, fireflies, droplets hanging in the air, the moon's reflection rippling. **Colours:** aqua and cyan body, cream-white frill, indigo night, silver moonlight, a warm gold shimmer on the water (a nod to the golden holo shimmer collectors praise on the real card); rainbow-gold full-art frame. **Why Grail:** level with Relaxo in every price source and ahead of it in some; one of the "Big Three".

**Ultra**

- *Sichlor* (Ultra, Pflanze, 072). **Pose:** airborne in an X-slash, both scythes crossed in front of the chest, wings a blur, eyes narrowed. **Scene:** a bamboo thicket cut clean along one diagonal, segments and leaves falling, shafts of light through the canopy. **Colours:** leaf green, cream belly, red eyes, pale gold light streaks against dark green depth. **Why:** wrapper star and a famous early tournament card (free retreat).
- *Flamara* (Ultra, Feuer, 073). **Pose:** sitting upright on a black volcanic rock, chest out, mane and tail fluffed like flames, breathing a spiral of fire upwards. **Scene:** jungle edge at sunset, smoke drifting through palm silhouettes, embers in the air. **Colours:** flame orange-red body, cream-yellow mane and tail, amber sky, charcoal rock. **Why:** wrapper star and one of the "Big Three".
- *Blitza* (Ultra, Elektro, 074). **Pose:** mid-leap towards the viewer, every spike bristled, a fan of needle-like bolts shooting out (its attack Pin Missile). **Scene:** thunderstorm over the canopy at night, lightning striking the tallest tree. **Colours:** electric yellow, white collar ruff, violet-black clouds, cyan-white lightning. **Why:** one of the "Big Three" and the highest-priced holo after the two Grails.
- *Tauboss* (Ultra, Farblos, 075). **Pose:** hovering in a mighty downstroke, talons forward, the long red-and-yellow crest plume streaming back. **Scene:** above the canopy, a hurricane spiral of leaves and wind lines below it, a river winding into the distance. **Colours:** warm tan-brown wings, cream breast, red-yellow crest, clear sky blue, white wind spirals. **Why:** the next market value after the Eeveelutions, and it completes the Basis-Set line Taubsi → Tauboga.
- *Knuddeluff* (Ultra, Farblos, 076). **Pose:** standing on a mossy log, both arms thrown up mid-cheer, ears bouncing, big open smile. **Scene:** behind it a row of small jungle animal silhouettes doing a stadium wave one after another (its attack Do the Wave); sunny clearing full of flowers. **Colours:** soft pink, cream belly, blue eyes, bright greens, sun yellow. **Why:** wrapper star.

## Community lore & meme material

- **Holo or twin:** Jungle was the first English set in which every Holo Rare also exists as a non-holo Rare with the same artwork (Pikawiz, PSA). The right Pokémon in the rare slot, but no shine: the schoolyard's first fake hit.
- **No Symbol:** all 16 Unlimited holos were also printed without the Jungle set symbol by mistake (Bulbapedia). The error copies are collected on their own; Elite Fourum even has a PSA 10 price-check thread for the No Symbol Vaporeon.
- **Sold out in two weeks:** the English 1st Edition run reportedly sold out within about two weeks of release (Bulbapedia, quoted by PSA in 2017).
- **The Big Three:** PSA's set-registry article calls Flareon, Jolteon and Vaporeon the set's "Big Three"; most price guides name Snorlax the chase card, with Vaporeon level or ahead in some snapshots.
- **Prerelease Clefable and the Raichu myth:** Jungle's prerelease card was Clefable with a "PRERELEASE" stamp. Bulbapedia says a few Base Set Raichu cards ended up on a test sheet of these Clefables and got the stamp too: the origin of the Prerelease Raichu (see the Basis-Set lore). Jungle Clefable now exists in four versions: 1st Edition, Unlimited, Prerelease, No Symbol (Elite Fourum guide).
- **W-Stamp Pikachu:** a promo version of the Jungle Pikachu carries a gold "W" stamp; listings link it to Wizards of the Coast and its magazine (?).
- **Wrapper nerds:** English packs show Wigglytuff, Scyther or Flareon; the Japanese pack shows a group of seven Pokémon and comes as a long and a short wrapper. Collectors on Elite Fourum compare tiny print differences between Unlimited Flareon wrappers.
- **Weighed packs:** the Base Set pack-weighing trick works on Jungle too. A Heritage lot of a sealed 1st Edition Jungle pack puts the weight in its title ("20.3 grams" (?)), and dealers advertise packs as "unweighed", "long crimp" or "Belgium".
- **Sealed displays:** US$7,200 (Heritage, 30 Apr 2020), US$14,900 (ComicConnect, 14 Dec 2020), US$12,000 (Collectrea, summer 2021); one price list reports US$96,000 in 2024, unconfirmed (?).
- **One Trainer, no Energy:** the whole set has a single Trainer, Poké Ball (flip a coin; heads, search your deck for a Pokémon), and no Energy at all, so Jungle packs never held an energy card.
- **Card texts as joke hooks** (English wording; German card texts not checked): Electrode's "Buzzap" knocks itself out to become an Energy card; Mr. Mime's "Invisible Wall" stops any attack of 30 or more damage; Snorlax is "Thick Skinned" and cannot be put to sleep, of all Pokémon; Meowth's attack "Pay Day" flips a coin to draw a card; Clefable's "Metronome" copies the opponent's attack; Wigglytuff's "Do the Wave" hits harder the fuller your bench; Nidoqueen's "Boyfriends" grows with every Nidoking in play; Cubone's "Snivel" weakens the opponent by crying.
- **Tournament memory:** Scyther (free retreat) and Mr. Mime joined the Base Set's Haymaker decks; Wigglytuff "Do the Wave" decks filled the bench on purpose (?).
- **Anime and game memes:** Pantimos keeps house for the hero's mother in the anime; Pummeluff sings everyone to sleep and then draws on their faces in a rage; Mauzi is Team Rocket's talking cat ("Jetzt gibt es Ärger!"); Relaxo blocks the road in the original games until someone plays the flute; Tragosso wears its mother's skull.

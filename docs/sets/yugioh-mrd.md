# Yu-Gi-Oh! — Metal Raiders (MRD)

> - **Origin (JP):** the TCG set merges two Japanese OCG packs: "Revival of Black Demons Dragon" (26 October 2000) and the Japanese "Metal Raiders" (22 February 2001, 84 cards, cover card Barrel Dragon). Yugipedia calls MRD the last early TCG core booster built from Series 1 cards.
> - **Release:** North America 26 June 2002, the second TCG booster after LOB. European English print (MRD-E) July 2003, **German 11 July 2003 (MRD-G)**, announced in a German article on 16 June 2003 ("Die Metal Raiders kommen nach Europa"). Asian English 24 October 2002 (?) and a Worldwide English print 1 December 2004 (?).
> - **Set size:** 144 cards (MRD-000 to MRD-143) = 86 Common, 12 Short Print, 2 Super Short Print, 22 Rare, 10 Super Rare, 10 Ultra Rare, 2 Secret Rare (Gate Guardian MRD-000, Thousand Dragon MRD-143). **The German print has all 144 cards under the same numbers**, unlike the shortened LOB-G.
> - **Booster:** 9 cards per pack. Pack artwork / cover card: **Black Skull Dragon** (the Japanese Metal Raiders pack showed Barrel Dragon).
> - **Display:** 24 boosters per box in retailer listings of Unlimited boxes; 1st Edition and German display size (?).
> - **MSRP then:** not found in this research (?). For reference, the 25th Anniversary reprint has an official MSRP of US$4.49 per pack.
> - **Market today:** sealed 1st Edition boxes sold for about US$4,450–6,500 on eBay in 2022 (PriceCharting). Guide values: Summoned Skull 1st Edition PSA 10 about US$1,600; Gate Guardian 1st Edition ungraded about US$116, a PSA 8 sold for US$199 in December 2024 (PriceCharting, SportsCardsPro).
> - **Print variants:** *1st Edition* ("1. Auflage") and *Unlimited*, as for LOB.
> - **Reprints:** Metal Raiders 25th Anniversary Edition (2023; MRD-EN in English, MRD-DE in German; exact date (?)).
> - **Out of print:** on 14 October 2003 Upper Deck, the TCG's distributor at the time, announced that the LOB, MRD, Magic Ruler (Spell Ruler), Pharaoh's Servant and Labyrinth of Nightmare boosters would be retired.

Tier logic, following LOB:

- **Grail (2):** the two biggest chases, one by each measure. Herbeigerufener Totenkopf (Summoned Skull) is Yugi's iconic monster and has the highest graded value of the set; Torwächter (Gate Guardian) is the MRD-000 Secret Rare with the highest raw value. Summoned Skull gets the higher weight (d 10 vs. 9).
- **Ultra (6):** the next chases by value and fame: the real cover card Schw. Totenkopfdrache, Überläufer, Spiegelkraft, Zeitzauberer, Feierliches Urteil and the second Secret Rare Tausenddrache (the payoff of the Time Wizard combo).
- **Holo (12):** the real Ultra Rares that are not top chases (Pistolendrache and three Counter Traps) and the most wanted Super Rares. The three Gate Guardian pieces stay together in Holo, like the Exodia limbs in LOB.
- **Rare (14):** the remaining Super Rares, the most popular real Rares, and Harpyie: a real Common, but Mai Valentine's signature card and priced like a Rare.
- **Uncommon (18) and Common (24):** real Short Prints, the other kept Rares and a few popular Commons are Uncommon; the rest is Common.
- **Cut (68 real cards):** 56 Commons, 6 Short Prints and 6 Rares: vanilla filler, incomplete fusions and minor effects (for example Kojikocy, Leogun, Labyrinth Tank, Giltia the D. Knight, Hoshiningen, Star Boy, Shield & Sword). Every Super Rare, Ultra Rare and Secret Rare is in. The most valuable cut card is Leghul (Short Print, about US$3.80).

## Card list

How to read `Real #/rarity`:

- `MRD-###` is the number and rarity in the 144-card North American list. The German print (MRD-G###), the European English print (MRD-E###) and the 2023 reprint (MRD-EN### / MRD-DE###) use the same numbers; spot-checked for MRD-G008, G009, G010, G018, G029, G033, G037, G057, G060 and G138.
- An Alternative row names the card whose artwork it replaces.

German names, attributes, card kinds, ATK/DEF and real rarities come from Konami's card database as served by the YGOPRODeck API (pulled in this session); key names were cross-checked against Yugipedia (see Open points). Desirability is our own estimate of collector and nostalgia pull inside this set, informed by current prices.

### German print (MRD-G)

- Released 11 July 2003 with all 144 cards under the North American numbers and rarities; nothing was cut or moved (LOB-G had lost 23 cards).
- Long names are abbreviated on the card, the same habit as "Blauäugiger w. Drache" in LOB: **Schw. Totenkopfdrache**, **Schw. Feuerdrache**, **Schloss d. dunklen Illusionen**.
- Some names are free translations: Harpie Lady is **Harpyie**, Summoned Skull **Herbeigerufener Totenkopf**, Change of Heart **Überläufer** ("defector"), Shadow Ghoul **Schatten-Leichenfledderer**, Roaring Ocean Snake **Brüllende Seeschnecke** (a roaring sea *snail*).

### Groups and fusion pairs inside this list

From the card texts (pulled through the API in this session):

- Torwächter is Special Summoned by tributing Sanga des Donners, Kazejin and Suijin (all three in Holo).
- Tausenddrache = Zeitzauberer + Babydrache.
- Schw. Totenkopfdrache = Herbeigerufener Totenkopf + Rotäugiger schwarzer Drache. The second material is in our LOB set, so this fusion spans two sets.
- Doppelköpfiger Donnerdrache = Donnerdrache + Donnerdrache.
- Bickuribox = Grober Clown + Traumclown.
- Musikerkönig = Hexe vom schwarzen Wald + Lady des Glaubens.
- Weevil's moth line: Kleine Motte wearing Kokon der Evolution becomes Große Motte; Mottenlarve belongs to the same line.
- Harpies: Vornehmer Egotist calls Harpyie or Harpyien-Schwestern from the deck.
- Incomplete in our list: Totenkopfritter (Verdorbene Weisheit + Antiker Geist) and Brüllende Seeschnecke (Mystische Lampe + Hyosube); their materials were cut as filler.

## Hero art briefs (Grail and Ultra)

Full art at 80×112 with at most about 32 colours (`ART_DIRECTION.md` §4); no text inside the art; the gold (Ultra) or rainbow-gold (Grail) treatment comes from the frame. Each hero should read at ×2 in the binder, so every brief names the one shape and the one light source that carry it.

**088 Herbeigerufener Totenkopf (Grail).**
- *Pose:* skeletal fiend in three-quarter view, crouched forward; both clawed hands raised to chest height with a lightning arc jumping between them; horned skull with glowing yellow eye sockets and open jaw; leathery wings flared up and out so they frame the head; ribcage worn like armour plates; thin tail curled around the feet.
- *Scene:* night storm over a ruined stone duel arena; two forked bolts in the sky echo the arc between the hands; broken pillars low in the frame.
- *Colours:* bone ramp (ivory to grey-brown), desaturated violet wing membranes, electric yellow-white lightning (the brightest value, only at the hands and the sky forks), indigo-to-black sky, small crimson accents at the wing joints.
- *Carries it:* the V of the wings and the bright arc between the hands.

**087 Torwächter (Grail).**
- *Pose:* towering three-part titan seen from a low angle: the base is the water spirit (Suijin) rising out of a whirlpool, the torso the golden thunder giant (Sanga) with arms crossed, the head the green wind mask (Kazejin) in a wind vortex, as on the real card, barring a huge stone gate.
- *Scene:* inside a stone labyrinth, maze walls converging on the gate arch; spray at the bottom, lightning around the torso, a wind swirl around the head.
- *Colours:* three bands from bottom to top: sea blue with foam white, gold-yellow, jade green; warm grey maze stone; dark violet sky.
- *Carries it:* three stacked colour bands, framed by the gate arch.

**086 Schw. Totenkopfdrache (Ultra, real cover card).**
- *Pose:* black dragon rearing on its hind legs, turned three-quarters toward the viewer, wings fully spread; its head wears a horned bone skull mask (a nod to Summoned Skull) with red eyes; jaws open, a stream of molten fire roaring toward the lower left corner.
- *Scene:* volcanic ridge at night, embers drifting up, red glow from below.
- *Colours:* charcoal-black ramp with violet rim light, ivory skull plates, molten orange-to-yellow fire, crimson eyes, dark red sky.
- *Carries it:* the fire stream and the skull are the only light areas; the spread wings read as a big W.

**085 Spiegelkraft (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* no character. A curved barrier of hexagonal mirror panels spans the frame; a thick orange monster beam hits it from the top right and is thrown back as a fan of rainbow shards.
- *Scene:* dark arena; two attacking monster silhouettes at the top recoil as the shards hit them.
- *Colours:* cyan-silver barrier ramp with white panel edges, a six-step rainbow for the shards, orange beam, deep navy background.
- *Carries it:* the barrier arc and the radial shards; keep the silhouettes flat and dark.

**084 Überläufer (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* young woman, waist-up, centred and split exactly down the middle: left half angel (white wing, halo arc, white dress, soft smile, blonde hair), right half devil (black bat wing, small horn, black dress, smirk, dark violet hair). One hand reaches out, beckoning; a thin ribbon of light from it leads to a small captured monster silhouette.
- *Scene:* the background splits along the same line: pale sky with clouds on the left, dark crimson with embers on the right.
- *Colours:* white and gold, black and crimson, one warm skin ramp, pastel blue.
- *Carries it:* one hard vertical split through figure and background.

**083 Zeitzauberer (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* small jester-like wizard in a purple robe and pointed cap, one foot kicked up mid-spell; one hand holds up a round clock-face shield (tick marks only, no numerals), the other a wand topped with a spinning roulette wheel.
- *Scene:* a vortex of floating clock faces and gears; a young sapling on one side and the same tree old and gnarled on the other (a thousand years in one picture).
- *Colours:* purple robe, gold gears and shield rim, teal vortex, white sparkles, a little green and brown for the trees.
- *Carries it:* the two bright discs, shield and wheel.

**082 Feierliches Urteil (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* ancient robed elder with a long white beard, seen slightly from below, arms raised high, palms open.
- *Scene:* storm clouds part and a column of white-gold light falls onto and through him; at his feet a monster silhouette and a few cards dissolve in the light.
- *Colours:* white-gold light ramp, royal blue robe, grey-violet clouds, warm skin.
- *Carries it:* the light column is the strongest value; the elder is a dark silhouette inside it with a bright rim.

**081 Tausenddrache (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* very old dragon sitting hunched, long white whiskers and beard trailing to the ground, drooping torn wings, wrinkled scales, a faint sparkling breath escaping its jaws.
- *Scene:* a stream of hourglass sand pouring down from the sky, ivy-covered ruins, and in the sand a tiny baby-dragon silhouette of what it once was.
- *Colours:* faded sage-green scales, cream beard, amber sand, dusky violet sky, moss green.
- *Carries it:* the long white beard and the falling sand.

## Community lore & meme material

Items marked (?) contain a detail that could not be confirmed in this session. Anime plot points are well known but were not re-checked against an episode guide.

- **Das Cover zeigt nicht den Grail.** The TCG pack shows Schw. Totenkopfdrache; the Japanese Metal Raiders pack showed Pistolendrache. Line: "Auf der Packung: ein Drache. In der Packung: Fröhlicher Sarg."
- **Drei Karten, ein Monster.** Torwächter needs Sanga des Donners, Kazejin and Suijin as tributes. In the anime it is the Paradox Brothers' boss in the Duelist Kingdom labyrinth tag duel against Yugi and Joey. Line: "Torwächter: drei Karten opfern für eine. Wie beim Amt: drei Formulare für einen Stempel."
- **Yugi + Joey = Schw. Totenkopfdrache.** The fusion of Yugi's Summoned Skull and Joey's Red-Eyes; in the anime the two fuse it during the Paradox Brothers duel (?). Its second material sits in LOB, so the fusion needs two sets.
- **Zeitzauberer.** The card flips a coin; the anime's "Time Magic" ages the field by a thousand years and turns Joey's Babydrache into Tausenddrache. Line: "Zeitzauberer: Ihr Anschlusszug kommt in 1.000 Jahren. Wir bitten um Verständnis."
- **The rarest Totenkopf.** A 2001 Asia Championship prize copy of Summoned Skull with a unique artwork is reportedly called the rarest card ever released in Konami's Master Guide 1 (?). The 2008 10th Anniversary Pack has a redraw by creator Kazuki Takahashi.
- **Kuriboh.** 300 ATK, more fans than most dragons. Yugi's Kuriboh plus Multiply wall is a famous anime moment (?), and Winged Kuriboh later became the GX hero's partner. Line: "Kuriboh: 300 ATK. 10.000 Fans. Mehr als du."
- **Mai Valentine's harpies.** Harpyie, Harpyien-Schwestern and Vornehmer Egotist are Mai's deck; in the anime she marks her cards with perfume to recognise them (?). Line: "Privatverkauf. Tierfreier Nichtraucherhaushalt. Karten riechen leicht nach Parfüm."
- **Weevil's moths.** Kleine Motte, Kokon der Evolution and Große Motte are Weevil Underwood's Duelist Kingdom line; the same Weevil throws Yugi's Exodia overboard (LOB lore).
- **Bandit Keith.** Pistolendrache is his ace; "In America!" from the parody *Yu-Gi-Oh! The Abridged Series* is a classic meme line (?).
- **Überläufer.** Change of Heart takes an enemy monster for a turn; the German name means "defector". It spent many years on the Forbidden list (?). In the anime the good Bakura helps Yugi through this card against his dark side (?). Staff event line: "Dein Praktikant hat Überläufer gespielt. Er arbeitet jetzt bei der Konkurrenz."
- **Spiegelkraft.** Mirror Force, the trap every schoolyard deck had: it destroys every attacking monster at once. Line: "Spiegelkraft. Heul leise."
- **Feierliches Urteil.** Solemn Judgment negates anything for half your Life Points. Line: "Feierliches Urteil: kostet die Hälfte. Wie das Finanzamt, nur schneller."
- **Hexe vom schwarzen Wald.** Witch of the Black Forest reads like a Black Forest witch. Line: "Hexe vom schwarzen Wald: sucht ein Monster, findet Kirschtorte." A long-time staple with ban-list history (?).
- **Bistro-Schlächter.** Döner gag: "Döner jetzt 8 €. Der Bistro-Schlächter hat gekündigt."
- **Name gems.** Fröhlicher Sarg, Schmerzen teilen, Abwehrratte, Brüllende Seeschnecke, Unglückliche Jungfrau: dark humour by name alone.
- **Jinzo #7 is not Jinzo.** PriceCharting lists MRD-035 simply as "Jinzo"; the real Jinzo came in a later set (Pharaoh's Servant (?)). Scam-listing gag: "Jinzo, 1. Auflage, Top-Zustand!!1 (#7)".
- **Super Short Prints.** Kokon der Evolution and Gazelle are Super Short Prints: commons that were harder to pull than their rarity says.
- **Germany got it complete.** After the LOB cut, German players got all 144 cards, 380 days after the US launch. Line: "380 Tage Verspätung. Dafür vollständig. Die Bahn wäre stolz."
- **Retired after three months.** Upper Deck announced the retirement of LOB, MRD and three later boosters on 14 October 2003, three months after the German release: a ready-made "Out of Print" event.

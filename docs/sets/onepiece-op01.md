# One Piece Card Game — OP-01 Romance Dawn

> - **Release:** Japan 22 July 2022 (the game itself launched two weeks earlier, on 8 July 2022, with Starter Decks ST-01…04) · English 2 December 2022 (boosters and ST-01…04 on the same day).
> - **Real set size:** 121 base cards (8 Leader, 45 C, 30 UC, 26 R, 10 SR, 2 SEC = 93 Characters, 20 Events, 8 Leaders, **0 Stages**) + 33 parallels = **154 printings**. 27 parallels are pack-inserted (8 Leader, 10 SR, 6 R, 2 SEC, 1 manga-art Shanks); 6 are box-topper only.
> - **Booster (EN):** 12 cards per booster · 24 boosters per display · 12 displays per case (retailer listings). The Japanese booster has only 6 cards (24 per box).
> - **Original MSRP:** US$4.19 per English booster (≈ US$100 per display; no official display MSRP found) · Japan ¥198 per pack / ¥4,752 per box.
> - **Booster artwork:** Monkey.D.Luffy key art on a red/white wrapper (?) — could not be verified in this research pass.
> - **Print runs:** the English first print ("Wave 1") is identified by a **blue box bottom** and is sold as "pre-errata"; the reprint ("Wave 2") has a **white bottom** (one source says black (?)). Sealed first-print displays trade at a multiple of reprint displays.
> - **Box topper:** every display holds one sealed topper pack with 1 of 6 parallel cards that cannot be pulled from boosters: Cavendish, Inuarashi, Nekomamushi, Alvida, Perona, Who's.Who.
> - **DON!! cards:** the game's resource cards (10-card DON!! deck per player; each launch Starter Deck ships 10). Whether every English OP-01 booster carries a DON!! card, and whether OP-01 already had alternate-art DON!! cards, is unverified (?).

Card data (numbers, names, rarities, colours, cost/power) was taken from the official English card database via the `punk-records` JSON mirror, so it reflects current errata text. The game uses its own numbering (`#`) and its own seven tiers; real rarities appear only in the `Real #/rarity` column.

Curation notes:

- **Leaders sit in Rare, not Holo.** The 10 SRs + 2 SECs already fill the 10–12 Holo slots, and real Leader cards are plentiful and cheap in non-parallel form. If Leaders should be Holo instead, Holo grows to 20 and Rare shrinks to 6.
- **Shanks and Yamato close the main block** (076/077) exactly as OP01-120/121 close the real set after Purple.
- **Colour blocks:** Red 001–022, Green 023–041, Blue 042–059, Purple 060–075. Dual-colour Leaders sit where the real set puts them (Law/Luffy with Red, Kaido/Crocodile with Blue).
- **Complete sub-collections kept on purpose:** all ten Straw Hats of the set, all nine Akazaya (Red Scabbards), every Warlord in the set, the Baroque Works numbers 1–3 plus Ms. All Sunday, and the Beast Pirates' three All-Stars and all six Tobi Roppo.
- **Omitted on purpose** (duplicate characters or low recognition): the second Jinbe (OP01-071), second X.Drake (OP01-054), character Doflamingo (OP01-073), Kyoshirou (= Denjiro), Hitokiri Kamazo (= Killer), Caesar Clown, Smiley, Krieg, Vergo, Monet, Mocha, Miss Doublefinger, Hyogoro, Shinobu, Otsuru, Penguin, Shachi, Jean Bart, Wire, Caribou, Sai, Hajrudin, Gordon, Komachiyo, the Kurozumi elders, the minor SMILE users and ten Events.
- **Unused real parallels** (reserve pool for events or a later "wave 2"): Leader parallels of Oden, Doflamingo, Kaido, Crocodile, King; SR parallels of Kin'emon, Law, Crocodile, Mihawk, King; R parallels of Sanji, Doflamingo, Ulti, Queen, Jack; the six box toppers.

## Community lore & meme material

- The set is named after Oda's "Romance Dawn" pilot one-shots and the manga's first chapter; the Japanese release date (22 July 2022) is the 25th anniversary of the manga's serialisation start (22 July 1997).
- The manga-art Shanks (OP01-120) is described by SNKRDUNK as the very first "manga parallel" ever printed for the game; later sets continued the manga-rare chase tier it started.
- Manga Shanks price trail (English, ungraded unless noted): PSA 9 sold for US$2,499 in December 2023; one raw sale hit US$1,995.97 in April 2024; raw eBay sales ran roughly US$564–1,000 during 2025; 2026 guides quote about US$1,330–1,480. Japanese copies are quoted anywhere from ¥138,000 to ¥320,000 depending on the source.
- Bandai publishes no pull rates. Community estimates for Japanese OP-01 boxes: 3–4 SR per box, 1–2 SR parallels per box, a Leader parallel every 2–3 boxes, a SEC every 4–6 boxes, a SEC parallel every 8–12 boxes, the special/manga slot every 12–15 boxes (?). For later sets a manga rare is estimated at about 1 per 72 boxes (6 cases).
- "Case" is an argument in itself: English OP-01 cases are sold as 12 displays, yet some pull-rate guides define a case as 6 boxes, so quoted "per case" odds can differ by a factor of two.
- First print versus reprint: English Wave 1 displays have a blue bottom, Wave 2 a white bottom. One 2026 guide puts sealed Wave 1 at about US$5,560 and Wave 2 at about US$1,787 (?) — against roughly US$100 at MSRP.
- First-print cards are marketed as "pre-errata": Bandai issued an "Up to" wording errata covering ST-01…04 and OP-01, and old cards must be played with the current official text.
- Six parallels (Cavendish, Inuarashi, Nekomamushi, Alvida, Perona, Who's.Who) exist only as box toppers — one random topper per display, so completing all six means buying at least six displays.
- The Trafalgar Law Leader parallel reportedly once peaked around US$2,000 (Dot Esports) (?) before settling near US$180 (CardGamer) — a textbook hype-and-crash chart.
- Luffy Leader parallel quotes range from US$72 and US$120 in older guides to US$1,162 in a 2026 retailer guide (?); nobody agrees what it is worth, only that everybody wants it.
- At the English launch the mono-red Zoro Leader deck was called "the number one most popular deck" (Twinfinite); its list ran four copies of the one-cost Nami.
- Nami OP01-016 is probably the most reprinted card of the set: the official English database lists further versions up to suffix `_p10`; her original parallel was quoted at US$137–247.
- The priciest card with an OP01 number is not from a booster: a "Top 8" tournament version of Zoro OP01-025 was listed around SGD 5,078 raw (TCGTalk, April 2026). In Japan a Flagship Battle prize Shanks is quoted above ¥800,000.
- Card text that writes its own jokes: Pacifista says "you may have any number of this card in your deck"; Yamato says "also treat this card's name as Kouzuki Oden"; SR Kaido can K.O. every other Character on the board for DON!! −6.
- No Thousand Sunny in boosters: OP-01 has zero Stage cards; the ship is a common in ST-01 and Onigashima Island a common in ST-04.
- Premium Booster PRB-01 "ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST" (Japan 27 July 2024, English 8 November 2024) reprinted Shanks OP01-120 including a manga-art version, restarting the "original or reprint?" debate.
- English players waited about four and a half months after Japan (22 July → 2 December 2022); Bandai has said it aims for simultaneous worldwide releases from 2026. A French edition only began with OP-09 on 7 February 2025.
- German fans say Ruffy, Lorenor Zorro, Lysop, Jimbei and Bartholomäus Bär while the cards say Luffy, Zoro, Usopp, Jinbe and Kuma; Carlsen calls the names an old editorial decision kept for consistency (GamePro). A newer German Netflix dub switched to the original names after 22 years and drew fan complaints, so "Ruffy oder Luffy?" is a ready-made shop-counter argument.
- Scalping still makes headlines: in July 2026 Weekly Shōnen Jump issue #33 sold out across Japan because of a bundled promo Luffy card (P-159); TSUTAYA stores held lottery sales, Shueisha apologised, reprinted the issue without the card and suspended card inserts.
- Launch-window scarcity, scalping and distributor allocation cuts for the English release in December 2022 are widely retold in the hobby (?) — no reliable source was found in this research pass.

## Open points

- **English booster wrapper artwork** not verified (assumed Luffy key art).
- **English pack collation** (how many commons/uncommons, hit slot, DON!! or Leader slot) not verified. Note the official product page is quoted with "1 pack: 6 cards", which matches the Japanese/Asia pack; the 12-card figure comes from US retailer listings (Walmart, StockX, others).
- **English pull rates** for OP-01 not verified; only Japanese community estimates were found, and sources disagree strongly on SEC and manga-rare frequency.
- **Launch scarcity, scalping, Bandai allocation issues in 2022** could not be sourced; the lore bullet is marked (?). The reprint/scalper coverage found dates from 2024–2026.
- **Reprint box bottom colour** (white per most sellers, black per one source) and whether all reprint cards carry corrected errata text.
- **Box-topper names:** Cavendish, Inuarashi, Nekomamushi and Alvida are confirmed by "Box Topper" listings; Perona (OP01-077) and Who's.Who (OP01-109) are inferred from a Japanese topper-pack listing of those numbers plus the official parallel list.
- **Case size:** 12 displays is based on retailer/eBay case listings, not on an official Bandai spec.
- **Display MSRP:** only the per-pack MSRP was found; US$100 is a StockX "retail price" and matches 24 × US$4.19.
- **All secondary-market prices** are snapshots from guides and price trackers that contradict each other; treat every figure as approximate.
- **Pack-inserted DON!! parallels in OP-01:** a Japanese shop lists an "OP01 DON!! parallel"; not confirmed for the English product.
- **German names** confirmed by search: Ruffy, Lorenor Zorro, Lysop, Jimbei, Bartholomäus Bär, Falkenauge. From general knowledge of the German edition, not re-verified: Tony Chopper, Sir Crocodile, Don Quichotte de Flamingo, Miss Bloody Sunday, Mr. 2 Bon Curry, Jazz Boner (marked (?)).
- **Design decisions to confirm:** Leaders in Rare instead of Holo; total of 89 cards (targets allow 82–90); Thousand Sunny left out of the booster list because it is not an OP-01 card (could be added as a starter-deck or promo card).

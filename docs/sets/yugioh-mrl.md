# Yu-Gi-Oh! — Spell Ruler (SRL, European MRL)

> - **Name:** in Japan "Magic Ruler". The English TCG renamed it **Spell Ruler** when Magic Cards became Spell Cards; the North American first print still used the set code MRL ("Magic Ruler"), the Worldwide print and the 2010 reprint use SRL. **In Germany it was released as "Spell Ruler" with set code SRL-G.** The European English boosters were also labelled Spell Ruler but their cards carry MRL-E codes. France: "Le Maître des Magies" (MDM-F), Italy: "Sovrano della Magia" (SDM-I), Spain: "Señor de Hechizos" (SDH-S). Our set id `yugioh-mrl` follows the European set ID; the shown name is Spell Ruler.
> - **Origin (JP):** merges the Japanese Series 2 boosters "Magic Ruler" (20 April 2000; cover cards Relinquished and Spellbinding Circle) and "Pharaoh's Servant" (date (?)). The first early TCG core booster not built from Series 1 cards.
> - **Release:** North America 16 September 2002, the third TCG booster. **German 21 October 2003 (SRL-G)**, French 22 October 2003, European English 24 October 2003 (MRL-E), Italian December 2003, Spanish 2004.
> - **Set size:** 104 cards in North America (SRL-000 to SRL-103) = 51 Common, 10 Short Print, 4 Super Short Print, 17 Rare, 10 Super Rare, 10 Ultra Rare, 2 Secret Rare (Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon SRL-000, Serpent Night Dragon SRL-103). **The European prints, German included, have 131 cards:** the 104 plus 27 cards that European LOB and the European Starter Decks had left out (see "German print" below).
> - **Booster:** 9 cards per pack (?). Pack artwork / cover card: **Relinquished**.
> - **Display:** 24 boosters (?).
> - **MSRP then:** not found in this research (?). The 2023 reprint is listed at US$4.49 per 9-card pack.
> - **Market today:** Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon is the clear top card: about US$75 for the Worldwide SRL print and US$200 or more for the first MRL-coded prints; next come the Ultra Rares Delinquent Duo (about US$32), Snatch Steal, Toon Mermaid and Toon Summoned Skull (US$18–21 each) (YGOPRODeck price data for the SRL prints, November 2026).
> - **Print variants:** *1st Edition* ("1. Auflage") and *Unlimited*.
> - **Reprints:** Legendary Collection (2010, North American Unlimited with SRL numbers); Spell Ruler 25th Anniversary Edition (first inside Legendary Collection: 25th Anniversary Edition, at retail 13–14 July 2023; SRL-DE in German).
> - **Out of print:** Upper Deck announced on 14 October 2003 that the LOB, MRD, Magic Ruler (Spell Ruler), Pharaoh's Servant and Labyrinth of Nightmare boosters would be retired, one week before the German release.

Tier logic, following LOB:

- **Grail (1):** Blauäugiger Toon-Drache, the SRL-000 Secret Rare, the most valuable card by far and Pegasus' cartoon copy of LOB's Grail.
- **Ultra (6):** the six most valuable real Ultra Rares, which include the real cover card Aufgegeben and the staple Mystischer Raum-Taifun: Verbrecher-Duo, Schnappstahl, Toon-Jungfrau, Herbeigerufener Toon-Schädel, Aufgegeben, Mystischer Raum-Taifun.
- **Holo (11):** the other four real Ultra Rares (Megawandler, Axt der Verzweiflung, Faszinierender Kreis, Starker Wachposten), the second Secret Rare Schlangen-Nachtdrache, and the Super Rares with the most fame or play history (Toon-Welt, Schwarze-Illusion-Ritual, Riesen-Trunade, Qual der Wahl, Beschlagnahme, Friedensbote).
- **Rare (13):** the remaining Super Rares (Schwarzer Anhänger, Maha Vailo, Thronangreifer, Verbanner des Lichts) and the best-known real Rares (Cyberdose, Mystische Tomate, Riesenkeim, Glänzender Elf, Senju der tausend Hände, Ufo-Schildkröte, Mutter Grizzly, Manga Ryu-Ran, Hyozanryu).
- **Uncommon (16):** the other real Rares, the kept (Super) Short Prints and the popular Commons Emporkömmling Goblin, Labyrinthmauern and the Hungriger Burger pair.
- **Common (21):** the rest, including two complete Ritual pairs and the funniest names.
- **Cut (36 real cards):** 26 Commons, 8 Short Prints and 2 Super Short Prints: vanilla filler and minor spells (for example Metal Fish, Queen Bird, Fire Kraken, Chain Energy, the five Short Print field spells). Every Rare and above is in. The most valuable cut card is Chain Energy (Common, about US$2.50).
- **European extras left out:** the 27 extra cards of the German print are not in this list (see "German print" and Open points).

## Card list

How to read `Real #/rarity`:

- `SRL-###` is the number and rarity in the 104-card North American / Worldwide list. The first North American print shows the same numbers as MRL-###, the European English print as MRL-E###, the German print as SRL-G### and the 2023 reprint as SRL-EN### / SRL-DE###. Spot-checked for SRL-G049, G072 and G088.
- Exception: the Secret Rare Serpent Night Dragon (SRL-103) moved to the end of the European list (MRL-E130), so its German number is **G130°** (derived from the European English list, not individually confirmed).
- An Alternative row names the card whose artwork it replaces.

German names, attributes, card kinds, ATK/DEF and real rarities come from Konami's card database as served by the YGOPRODeck API (pulled in this session); key names were cross-checked against Yugipedia (see Open points). Desirability is our own estimate of collector and nostalgia pull inside this set, informed by current prices.

### German print (SRL-G): the 27 extra cards

- Released 21 October 2003 with 131 cards (SRL-G000 to SRL-G130; a German retailer lists 131 cards and SRL-G129 as a Rare). SRL-G000–G102 match the North American numbers.
- SRL-G103–G129 hold 27 cards that European LOB and the European Starter Decks had left out. Per the European English list (MRL-E103–E129): Biberkrieger, Mammutfriedhof, Neo, magischer Schwertkämpfer, Menschenfresser-Schatztruhe, Hitotsu-Me-Riese, Ookazi, Bleiche Bestie, Hane-Hane, Flammen-Schwertkämpfer, Strafe des Albtraums, Verschmelzer, Kleiner Engel, Mystisches Schaf #2, Masaki, legendärer Schwertkämpfer, Kurama, Macht von Kaishin, Berg, Hinotama, Karbonala-Krieger, Larvas, M-Krieger #1, M-Krieger #2, Rasender Panda, Böse Drachenkämpferin, Einäugiger Schilddrache, Sandstein, Topf der Gier. All are Commons except Böse Drachenkämpferin and Topf der Gier (both Rare). The German numbers are derived from the European English list.
- 23 of them are the cards cut from European LOB (Sandstein is the 23rd card the LOB research could not identify). The other four are starter-deck cards: Neo, magischer Schwertkämpfer and Menschenfresser-Schatztruhe (Starter Deck Yugi), Ookazi and Bleiche Bestie (Starter Deck Kaiba); Bleiche Bestie is also an MRD card.
- **Our list leaves all 27 out:** the LOB cards are already in our LOB set, which uses the full 126-card pool as the 2023 German LOB reprint does, and none of the four starter-deck cards is a chase. Duplicating Topf der Gier or Flammen-Schwertkämpfer across two sets would confuse collectors.

### Naming notes

- Snatch Steal is **Schnappstahl**: "steal" read as "Stahl" (steel).
- Relinquished is **Aufgegeben** (literally "given up").
- Shining Angel is **Glänzender Elf**: the angel became an elf (confirmed for SRL-G088).
- Toon Mermaid is **Toon-Jungfrau**: the official name drops the "Meer"; German sellers still write "Toon Meerjungfrau" (confirmed for SRL-G072).
- Toon Summoned Skull is **Herbeigerufener Toon-Schädel**, while the original in MRD is "Herbeigerufener **Totenkopf**".
- Cyber Jar is **Cyberdose** (a tin can), Upstart Goblin **Emporkömmling Goblin**, Malevolent Nuzzler **Übelwollender Hätschler**, Spear Cretin **Speer-Idiot**, Labyrinth Wall **Labyrinthmauern** (plural).

### Groups inside this list

From the card texts (pulled through the API in this session):

- Toons: Toon-Welt (pay 1000 LP) is the basis for Blauäugiger Toon-Drache, Herbeigerufener Toon-Schädel, Toon-Jungfrau and Manga Ryu-Ran; Ryu-Ran is the non-Toon original.
- Rituals, all four pairs complete: Aufgegeben with Schwarze-Illusion-Ritual, Hungriger Burger with Hamburger-Rezept, Krebsschildkröte with Schildkrötenschwur, Auftritt des Schwerts with Eröffnungstanz.
- The Paradox Brothers' maze: Labyrinthmauern, Mauerschatten and Magisches Labyrinth (their Gate Guardian is in MRD).

## Hero art briefs (Grail and Ultra)

Full art at 80×112 with at most about 32 colours (`ART_DIRECTION.md` §4); no text inside the art; the gold (Ultra) or rainbow-gold (Grail) treatment comes from the frame. **Toon heroes** (080, 077, 074) use a Toon look so they read as cartoons next to every other hero: flat fills, two shading steps at most, thick dark outlines, exaggerated eyes.

**080 Blauäugiger Toon-Drache (Grail).**
- *Pose:* cartoon white dragon bursting out of an open pop-up book toward the viewer, rubbery neck stretched, huge sparkly blue eyes, wide goofy grin with a tongue, little wings flapping inside motion lines.
- *Scene:* the open book fills the bottom of the frame; cartoon clouds, starbursts and paper scraps fly around it; bright yellow sky.
- *Colours:* white-to-pale-blue dragon, sky yellow, primary red and blue accents, thick dark outline.
- *Carries it:* the Toon style itself, the giant eyes and the book; it must read as the cartoon twin of LOB's Blue-Eyes at a glance.

**079 Aufgegeben (Ultra, real cover card).**
- *Pose:* small bone-white humanoid crouched on a dark altar, leathery wings folded; its face is a single large stylised eye with a tear line (our own glyph); one clawed hand outstretched, pulling a ghostly captured monster into its body.
- *Scene:* black-violet void with a giant faint eye glyph behind the figure, a few ritual candles.
- *Colours:* bone-white ramp, violet void, gold eye glyph, ghost teal for the captured monster.
- *Carries it:* the contrast of scale: tiny figure, huge eye behind it.

**078 Mystischer Raum-Taifun (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* no character. A green-white cyclone spirals from the upper left into the centre, its tail sweeping across the frame; at its eye a face-down card tears into shards.
- *Scene:* deep space with nebula clouds; the stars bend into the spiral.
- *Colours:* emerald ramp, white core, violet nebula, black-blue space, pinprick stars.
- *Carries it:* one big spiral; the card shards give it scale.

**077 Herbeigerufener Toon-Schädel (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* cartoon skull demon mid-jump, bendy noodle arms wide, goofy grin with the tongue out, zigzag cartoon lightning from its fingertips.
- *Scene:* comic-panel background with halftone dots and an action starburst behind it.
- *Colours:* lavender-grey bone, yellow lightning, red tongue, cyan sky, thick dark outline.
- *Carries it:* the starburst behind a goofy silhouette; it parodies MRD's Grail the way the Toon dragon parodies LOB's.

**076 Schnappstahl (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* a red-gloved hand with a chain reaches in from the bottom right; the chain is looped around the collar of an enemy monster (a generic beast warrior) and drags it across the frame toward the viewer.
- *Scene:* steel-grey background of interlocking gears with the open jaws of a steel trap: the "Stahl" pun of the German name.
- *Colours:* steel-grey ramp, bronze chain highlights, dull green monster, red glove.
- *Carries it:* the diagonal chain line.

**075 Verbrecher-Duo (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* two little imps back to back, one red with a pointed tail, one blue with bat ears, each snatching a card out of a fanned hand held up at the frame edges; cheeky grins.
- *Scene:* night alley under a single street lamp.
- *Colours:* red and blue imps, yellow lamp light, dark navy alley, white cards.
- *Carries it:* two colour-coded silhouettes under one light cone.

**074 Toon-Jungfrau (Ultra).**
- *Pose:* cartoon mermaid with big eyes and a red bow, sitting on a rock and waving, her tail flicking up a heart-shaped splash.
- *Scene:* cartoon sea with curly waves and a smiling sun.
- *Colours:* teal tail, pink hair, red bow, yellow sun, two blues for the sea, thick dark outline.
- *Carries it:* the heart-shaped splash and the round Toon eyes.

## Community lore & meme material

Items marked (?) contain a detail that could not be confirmed in this session. Anime plot points are well known but were not re-checked against an episode guide.

- **Ein Set, sechs Namen.** Magic Ruler in Japan, Spell Ruler in English and German, Le Maître des Magies, Sovrano della Magia, Señor de Hechizos; the cards say MRL, SRL, MRL-E or SRL-G depending on print. Line: "Ein Set, sechs Namen, vier Kürzel. Passierschein A38 gibt's an Schalter 7."
- **Magic wird Spell.** The rename came with the switch from "Magic Cards" to "Spell Cards", usually linked to Wizards of the Coast's "Magic" trademark (?).
- **Germany got the leftovers.** The German Spell Ruler carries 27 extra cards, among them Topf der Gier as a Rare and Flammen-Schwertkämpfer as a Common: German players first pulled Pot of Greed in Spell Ruler, not in LOB. Line: "Topf der Gier? Kommt im nächsten Set. Vielleicht."
- **400 Tage.** North America 16 September 2002, Germany 21 October 2003, one week after Upper Deck had announced the booster's retirement. Line: "In Deutschland erschienen, in Amerika schon ausgemustert. Willkommen im Lieferverzug."
- **Schnappstahl.** "Snatch Steal" became snatch-steel. Line: "Schnappstahl: klaut dein Monster. Rostfrei." Snatch Steal and Verbrecher-Duo both spent years on the Forbidden list (?).
- **Aufgegeben.** Relinquished, translated literally. Lines: "Aufgegeben. Wie dein Sparplan." · "Pull: Aufgegeben. Mutti: 'Das sagst du immer.'"
- **Glänzender Elf.** The Shining Angel lost its wings in translation, at least in name.
- **Pegasus und die Toons.** Toon-Welt costs 1000 LP; in the anime Pegasus copies Kaiba's Blue-Eyes as a Toon, which makes our Grail the cartoon twin of LOB's Grail. Line: "Blauäugiger Toon-Drache: wie der echte, nur mit Lachen vom Band."
- **Pegasus' ritual.** Aufgegeben with Schwarze-Illusion-Ritual is Pegasus' card from the Duelist Kingdom final against Yugi; its eye face echoes his Millennium Eye (?).
- **Hungriger Burger.** A hamburger with teeth, summoned by a ritual from a recipe; a long-running community joke. Lines: "Hamburger-Rezept: Ritualzauber. Ein Döner wäre billiger gewesen." · "Hungriger Burger eröffnet neben deiner Döner-Bude. Preiskrieg."
- **Cyberdose.** Cyber Jar flips every monster and was banned for years (?). Line: "Cyberdose. Kein Pfand."
- **Beschlagnahme und Tribut.** Confiscation looks at your hand and takes a card; Toll makes every attack cost Life Points. Line: "Herr Griesgram vom Finanzamt spielt Beschlagnahme. Dann Tribut. Dann Mittagspause."
- **Qual der Wahl.** Painful Choice: you pick five cards, your opponent picks the one you keep. Long Forbidden (?). Line: "Qual der Wahl: du suchst aus, der andere entscheidet. Wie Urlaub mit den Eltern."
- **Mutter Grizzly.** Line: "Mutter Grizzly fragt, ob du schon gegessen hast. Dann holt sie ein WASSER-Monster aus deinem Deck."
- **Name gems.** Speer-Idiot, Übelwollender Hätschler, Haus der Klebestreifen, Oktoberser, Spielautomat, Emporkömmling Goblin. Line: "Oktoberser: acht Arme, acht Maß."
- **Spielautomat.** Slot Machine is a Super Short Print, harder to pull than a Rare. Line: "Spielautomat: Super Short Print. Gewonnen hast du trotzdem nichts."
- **Das Labyrinth der Paradox-Brüder.** Labyrinthmauern, Mauerschatten and Magisches Labyrinth are the Paradox Brothers' maze from the anime; their Gate Guardian sits one set earlier in MRD. Line: "Wer das Duell nachspielen will, braucht zwei Sets. Wie beim Möbelhaus: die Schrauben sind im anderen Karton."
- **Schlangen-Nachtdrache.** The second Secret Rare, Rex Raptor's dragon in the anime (?).

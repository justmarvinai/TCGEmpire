# One Piece Card Game — OP-02 Paramount War

> - **Release:** Japan 4 November 2022 · English 10 March 2023 (date on the official English product page, as quoted in search results; some retailers list later dates).
> - **Real set size:** 121 base cards (8 Leader, 45 C, 30 UC, 26 R, 10 SR, 2 SEC = 93 Characters, 16 Events, 4 Stages) + 33 parallels = **154 printings**. The parallels are 8 Leader, 10 SR, 2 SEC, 6 R, the manga-art Ace and the 6 box-topper parallels.
> - **Colours:** Red (Whitebeard Pirates), Green (Wano, Minks, the Straw Hats in their *Film Red* outfits), Blue (the Impel Down breakout and the Warlords), Purple (the Impel Down staff and film villains) and **Black for the first time** (the Navy). Dual Leaders: Garp Red/Black, Sanji Green/Blue, Zephyr Purple/Black.
> - **Booster (EN):** 12 cards per booster · 24 boosters per display (retailer listings). The Japanese pack has 6 cards, 24 packs per box.
> - **Original MSRP:** US$4.19 per English booster (official product page as quoted; no official display price found, 24 × 4.19 ≈ US$100) · Japan ¥198 per pack (one Japanese source; tax status unclear) (?).
> - **Booster artwork:** not verified (?); our picks for the game are under "Booster wrappers".
> - **Box topper:** every display holds one sealed topper pack with 1 of 6 parallels that are not in boosters: Squard, Kouzuki Toki, Boa Hancock, Minokoala, Tashigi, Donquixote Rosinante (numbers 009, 031, 059, 086, 105, 108 from a Japanese topper-pack listing; names from the official card data).
> - **Pull rates:** Bandai publishes none. Community estimate for Japanese boxes: manga Ace about 1 in 72 boxes; one guide says a SEC about every 3 boxes (?).
> - **In the game:** the booster can copy OP-01 (12 cards: 7 Common, 3 Uncommon, 1 Rare, 1 hit slot; `SETS.md` §3).

Card data (numbers, names, rarities, colours, cost/power, effect texts) was taken from the official English card database via the `punk-records` JSON mirror (`english/data/569102.json`), so it reflects current errata text. The game uses its own numbering (`#`) and its own seven tiers; real rarities appear only in the `Real #/rarity` column.

Curation notes:

- **Leaders sit in Rare, not Holo,** as in OP-01. Rare holds the 8 Leaders and the six most wanted real Rares (Marco, the FILM Luffy, Yamato, Buggy, Koby, Garp).
- **One card per character, as in OP-01:** the SR Kuzan (OP02-096) is left out because the SEC Kuzan is the better-known card, so Holo has 11 cards, not 12. The one exception is Luffy: the SR (Impel Down, Blue) and the FILM Rare (Green) are both in, so the *Film Red* crew stays complete. The Rare carries our suffix "(FILM)" so every name stays unique; the frame strips the suffix as it does "(Leader)".
- **Colour blocks:** Red 001–013, Green 014–030, Blue 031–044, Purple 045–053, Black 054–067. Uta (Purple) and Kuzan (Black) close the main block at 068/069, as OP02-120/121 close the real set. Dual-colour Leaders sit where the real set puts them.
- **Complete sub-collections kept on purpose:** all ten Straw Hats in *Film Red* outfits (Sanji as Leader) plus Law; the Impel Down breakout (Luffy, Buggy, Mr.1, Mr.2, Mr.3, Crocodile, Ivankov; Jinbe is in Green); every Warlord in the set (Mihawk, Moria, Doflamingo, Kuma, Hancock) plus ex-Warlord Crocodile; the three Admirals (Sakazuki, Borsalino, Kuzan) with Fleet Admiral Sengoku and both Garps; the Impel Down staff (Magellan, Hannyabal, Sadi, Shiryu).
- **Stages left out:** OP-02 brings the first four booster Stages (Moby Dick, Land of Wano, New Kama Land, Impel Down), but `CardKind` has no stage and the One Piece frame would print EVENT on them. With a `stage` kind, Moby Dick is the one to add.
- **Omitted on purpose** (vanilla or low recognition, duplicates, Stages): Atmos, Kingdew, Thatch, Dogura, Blenheim, Whitey Bay, Magura, Rakuyo, LittleOars Jr., Inuarashi and Nekomamushi (both already in OP-01), Shishilian, Wanda, Inazuma, Cabaji, Mohji, Morley, Saldeath, Solitaire, Daifugo, Douglas Bullet, Dobon, Domino, Blugori, Minokoala, Minotaur, Sphinx, Isuka, Onigumo, the SR Kuzan, Komille, Jango, Strawberry, Sentomaru, Tsuru, Doberman, Fullbody, Helmeppo, Yamakaji, the four Stages and the Events Whitebeard Pirates, Diable Jambe Venaison Shoot, Paradise Totsuka, Impel Down All Stars, Arabesque Brick Fist, Gum-Gum Rain, Hydra, Venom Road and Yasakani Sacred Jewel (52 cards).
- **Unused real parallels** (reserve pool for events or a later wave): Leader parallels of Kin'emon, Ivankov, Magellan, Zephyr; SR parallels of Ivankov, Magellan and the SR Kuzan; R parallels of Masked Deuce and Little Sadi; the box toppers Squard, Kouzuki Toki, Minokoala.

## Grail and Ultra art briefs (hand-drawn, full art)

Each of these is crafted by hand (up to four review rounds, `ART_DIRECTION.md`). Foil shines on the frame and the artwork, never under the text plates; no text is baked into the art.

- **090 Portgas.D.Ace [manga art]** (Grail, Red). *Pose:* chest-up, three-quarter turn, Ace mid-laugh with eyes shut, right fist raised and swallowed by a flame that bursts upward out of the frame; his bare back half-turned so the crew mark on it shows. *Scene:* a black-and-white manga page: two or three inset panels behind him (a burning ship, his hat in close-up), white gutters, speed lines and screentone dots for smoke and fire. *Colours:* the art is strictly black, white and four greys; colour lives only on the frame (gold foil) and the red name bar. It is the only greyscale artwork in the set, and that contrast is the point.
- **089 Portgas.D.Ace [alt art]** (Ultra, Red). *Pose:* sitting on a ship's rail, one knee up, grinning with his eyes closed, orange hat with its two little faces tipped back; flames rise off his shoulders and twist into a crown above his head. *Scene:* sunset sea, two gulls, a thin trail of fire on the water. *Colours:* orange and red fire, pink-violet sunset, deep blue sea, freckles as single darker pixels.
- **084 Edward.Newgate [Leader, alt art]** (Ultra, Red). *Pose:* enormous, seated on the whale-head figurehead of his ship, bisento across his knees, chin up, crescent moustache catching the light, coat over his shoulders billowing. *Scene:* the white ship breaking through a wave, crew silhouettes raising weapons behind him, the sky cracked like glass by his quake power. *Colours:* bone-white coat and moustache, black bandana, red sky shading to orange, the cracks in pale cyan carry the foil.
- **085 Monkey.D.Garp [Leader, alt art]** (Ultra, Red/Black). *Pose:* punching straight at the viewer, fist huge in the foreground, white coat flaring behind, wide grin, grey beard. *Scene:* night sky; the punch bends a spiral galaxy around the fist, a broken warship silhouette far below. *Colours:* split background along the frame seam, red on the left and black on the right; violet-blue galaxy, white stars.

## Community lore & meme material

- **The name:** the Japanese set is 頂上決戦 ("Chōjō Kessen", roughly "the decisive battle at the summit"). "Paramount War" is the English anime's name for the Marineford war; the English manga calls it the "Summit War" (?).
- **Black arrives:** OP-02 is the first booster with Black cards, the Navy. Black's trick is to cut a Character's cost to 0 and then K.O. everything that costs 0 (Garp, Kuzan SEC). Joke hook: "Erst auf null runterstufen, dann entsorgen. Marine-Bürokratie als Spielprinzip."
- **The Grail:** the manga-art Ace is the set's "comic parallel" and shows Ace at Marineford. Community estimate: about 1 in 72 Japanese boxes, i.e. six cases for one card.
- **Manga Ace price trail (English, ungraded unless noted):** CardGamer lists US$605; recent PriceCharting sales ran US$366–650; Samurai Sword Tokyo quotes about US$1,195 (March 2026); TCGTalk SGD 1,836 raw (April 2026). Nobody agrees, everybody wants it.
- **The reprint costs more than the original:** Premium Booster PRB-01 reprinted the manga Ace; one Japanese shop asked ¥650,000 for the reprint and ¥140,000 for the OP-02 original (single-shop asking prices) (?).
- **Whitebeard hurts himself on purpose:** his Leader text reads "[End of Your Turn] Add 1 card from the top of your Life cards to your hand", so he takes a Life card every turn. The Event named after his words to Ace, "You May Be a Fool...but I Still Love You", is the card that stops him. Joke hook: "Haut sich jede Runde selbst eine rein. Nennt das Strategie."
- **Moby Dick only sails when you are nearly dead:** the Stage (not in our list) gives Whitebeard Pirates +2000 power only with 1 or fewer Life cards. "Das Schiff legt erst ab, wenn du schon halb tot bist."
- **Byrnndi World:** "DON!! −8: This Character gains +792000 power during this turn", an 800,000-power Character for one turn. The biggest number in our One Piece lists. "800.000 Power. Kostet nur deine komplette Runde."
- **The six box toppers:** one random topper per display, so the full set of six needs at least six displays. Boa Hancock is the one people want (about US$12 raw); nobody opens a display hoping for Minokoala.
- **Leaders beat the SRs:** the Whitebeard Leader parallel (US$80–132) and the Smoker Leader parallel (about US$88 raw, PSA 10 about US$155) out-price most SR parallels; the Ace SR parallel sits near US$40, Sanji's Leader parallel at US$33–45.
- **Uta, a film star in a war set:** the *Film Red* singer is a Secret Rare in a Marineford set. Her SEC parallel trades for only about US$13–49 raw (PSA/BGS 10 about US$55–140). "Weltstar im Kino, Wühltisch im Binder."
- **The priciest Kuzan is not in a booster:** the Championship prize Kuzan is quoted at about SGD 6,304 in Japanese and SGD 1,876 in English (TCGTalk); the SEC parallel from boosters costs about US$12–25.
- **Impel Down, the collection:** the breakout crew (Luffy, Buggy, Mr.1, Mr.2, Mr.3, Crocodile, Ivankov, Jinbe) is nearly all in the set. Magellan, the warden, spends about ten hours a day on the toilet because of his poison diet (manga), while vice warden Hannyabal openly wants his job. "Der Chef sitzt auf dem Klo, der Stellvertreter sägt am Stuhl. Wie im Amt."
- **Koby's scream:** the young marine who threw himself in front of an Admiral and screamed at everyone to stop fighting; Shanks arrived a moment later and ended the war (manga). "Ein Schrei, und plötzlich hören alle zu."
- **Buggy, famous by accident:** the broadcast from Marineford made him a star among pirates without him doing anything (manga). "Zufällig im Fernsehen, sofort Promi."
- **German names:** in the German manga Ace is "Puma D. Ace" (?), so the card says Portgas and the fan says Puma; "Ruffy oder Luffy?" from OP-01 works here too.

## Open points

- **English booster wrapper artwork** not verified; the official product page could not be fetched and no search result described it.
- **English pack collation** (12 cards per pack from retailer listings; Leader or DON!! slot) and whether every pack holds a DON!! card are unverified.
- **Japanese price:** ¥198 per pack comes from one Japanese article; tax status and box price unverified.
- **English release date:** 10 March 2023 per the official page as quoted in search results; some retailers list 31 March 2023 or February 2023.
- **Box toppers:** Boa Hancock and Minokoala are confirmed by "Box Topper" listings; the six numbers come from a Japanese topper-pack listing and the names from the official card data.
- **Pull rates:** community estimates only; one guide gives a SEC about every 3 boxes, another version of the same guide gives no figure.
- **Case size:** 12 displays per case is assumed from OP-01 and a sealed Japanese OP-03 case of 12 boxes; no OP-02 case listing was checked.
- **PRB-01 reprint prices** (¥650,000 for the reprinted manga Ace against ¥140,000 for the original) are one Japanese shop's asking prices, not sales.
- **All prices are snapshots** from guides and price trackers that contradict each other. The Garp Leader parallel is unclear: one sale labelled as it at about US$25, foil sales of US$113–220 may be other versions.
- **German names** "Puma D. Ace", "Senghok" (Sengoku) and "Don Quichotte de Rosinante" are from general knowledge and were not re-verified (the search budget ran out); the names already used in OP-01 are copied from there.
- **"Paramount War" vs. "Summit War"** naming (anime dub vs. English manga) is from general knowledge (?).
- **Design decisions to confirm:** one Grail (candidate for a second: the Whitebeard Leader parallel); the Garp Leader parallel as sixth Ultra (alternative: the Sanji Leader parallel); the SR Kuzan dropped; the "(FILM)" suffix for the second Luffy; the four Stages left out until there is a `stage` kind; 90 cards (the maximum).
- **Art pipeline:** `Schwarz` is a new One Piece colour in the content. The frame has a hue for it (`art/frames/onepiece.ts`), but the kit scenes (`art/kit/scene.ts`) cover only Rot, Grün, Blau and Lila, so a Schwarz scene is needed before the batch.
- **Research limits:** page fetches were blocked (official site, PriceCharting, TCGTalk); the web-search budget ran out before the last checks (German names, wrapper art, starter deck dates).

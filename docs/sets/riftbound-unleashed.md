# Riftbound — Unleashed (UNL)

> **Publisher / codes:** Riot Games (English-language production and distribution partner: UVS Games) · Set 3 "Unleashed" = `UNL` · Champion Decks: Vi and Vex. (Set 4 "Vendetta" followed on 31 Jul 2026 and set 5 "Radiance" on 23 Oct 2026; neither is part of this file.)
> **Release:** Mainland China 10 Apr 2026 (Pre-Rift events from 3 Apr) · English global launch 8 May 2026 (North America, LATAM, EMEA, Oceania; Pre-Rift events 1–7 May) · Summoner Skirmish store events from 25 May and 22 Jun.
> **Real set size:** 219 numbered cards: six domain blocks of 30 (Fury 001–030, Calm 031–060, Mind 061–090, Body 091–120, Chaos 121–150, Order 151–180; each 10 Common, 8 Uncommon, 8 Rare, 4 Epic), 181–204 the 12 Legends (Rare, odd numbers) with their 12 signature cards (even numbers; two of them units), 205–219 fifteen Battlefields (Uncommon) · plus the six basic Runes R01–R06 (?). Showcase: alternate art (`a`) for champion units and jungle monsters (count (?)), 19 Overnumbered (220–225: six Poros; 226–237: the 12 Legends; 238: Baron Nashor, the first card of the new "Ultimate" rarity) and 12 Signatures (226\*–237\*, the Legends). Database totals: 300 (riftbound.org), 308 (TCGodds, RareTracker), 310 (TCGIndex). Riot's launch release said "220+ cards".
> **Booster:** 14 cards (one retailer says 12 (?)); 7 Commons confirmed, the rest assumed as in Origins and Spiritforged (3 Uncommon, 2 foil Rare-or-better, 1 foil of any rarity, 1 token-or-rune slot) (?) · pack MSRP (?) · **Display:** 24 boosters · MSRP US$119.99 / €127.99 (Riot Merch Store).
> **Pull rates:** Riot: Baron Nashor (Ultimate) appears in under 0.1 % of packs (under 1 in 1,000), never more than one per pack. Community estimates (TCGTalk, TCGodds; small samples): Overnumbered ≈ 1.7 % of packs (≈ 1 per 2–3 displays), alternate art 2–4 per display (≈ 10 % of packs), Epics 6–7 per display. "1 in 10 Overnumbered is signed" is assumed from Origins (?).
> **New mechanics:** XP (champions level up), Ambush, Hunt, token battlefields. Theme: the jungle of Summoner's Rift (Riot: "Step into the jungle") with its monsters: Baron Nashor, Elder Dragon, Rift Herald, the red and blue buff monsters, Scuttle Crab, Gromp, and Smite as a card.
> **Launch line-up:** Booster, Display, Champion Decks Vi (Piltover Enforcer) and Vex (Gloomist) at US$19.99 each (56 cards), the Unleashed Vault (US$34.99: 6 boosters, 36 basic runes, 3 double-sided full-art tokens, 2 punch-out dividers), sleeved boosters in three designs (?), Pre-Rift Event Kit (trade, MSRP US$480). Display box art: Vi, Rengar, Master Yi and LeBlanc ("a teamfight gank", Riot).
> **Supply at launch:** From Unleashed on Riot's merch store runs two drawing windows per set (one before launch, one after) with expanded bot and scalper checks. Retail displays sold above MSRP at launch (US$150–170 at some shops). The Atlanta Regional Qualifier drew more than 1,800 players the weekend before launch.

**Which set is "the third".** Riot's order is Origins (set 1), Spiritforged (set 2), Unleashed (set 3), Vendetta (set 4, 31 Jul 2026), Radiance (set 5, 23 Oct 2026). Unleashed has been out since May 2026 and is well documented, so no announced-only data was needed.

**Naming.** As in Origins, a Legend prints only its title with the champion as a tag; databases write "Diana, Scorn of the Moon", which is the form used here. `a` cards are "alternate art", `220+` cards "Overnumbered", `*` cards "Signature" (the Overnumbered art plus the artist's printed signature), and the Baron is sold as "Baron Nashor (Ultimate)". All names are the official English names; no German card print could be confirmed (?).

**Why two Grails.** The set has two equal top chases. Baron Nashor (Ultimate) is Riftbound's first Ultimate card and the face of the jungle set: market US$1,390 (riftbound.org) to US$1,551 (TCG Watchtower) to US$1,799 (TCGIndex), completed sales US$1,261–1,869 in late Oct / early Nov 2026 (Crypto.com), PSA 10 about US$2,618. Diana, Scorn of the Moon (Signature) is the most valuable Signature: US$1,311 (TCG Watchtower) to US$1,530 (Oct 2026 snapshots). Baron gets the higher weight (d 10 vs 9): it is the rarer pull and the more iconic card.

**Ultra picks.** The next six Signatures by price (two snapshots, Oct–Nov 2026): LeBlanc, Deceiver (US$965–989), Vex, Gloomist (US$633–653), Vi, Piltover Enforcer (US$629–641), Jhin, Virtuoso (US$604–617), Pyke, Bloodharbor Ripper (US$567–589), Rengar, Pridestalker (US$543–573). Left out: Lillia (US$491–511), Master Yi (US$445–457), Poppy (US$422–428), Kha'Zix (US$395–422), Ivern (about US$400). Lillia is close behind Rengar and was one of the four champions Riot announced first; swap them if the owner prefers.

**Tier rule deviations.**

- Lonely Poro (Overnumbered) is an Alternative whose base card is the Spiritforged common (our Spiritforged 024), not an Unleashed main-set card; the Overnumbered printing exists only in Unleashed packs. It is the priciest of the six Overnumbered Poros (US$231–331 vs about US$170–205) and pure meme fuel.
- Baron Nashor sits in three tiers, as in the real packs: the Epic (about US$20–25) in Rare, its alt art (about US$20) in Alternative, the Ultimate in Grail.
- Master Yi has no Alternative: no alt-art number was found for either of his units.
- Ultra and Grail Legends are Signatures as in Origins; the second Grail is a unit (the Ultimate Baron).
- Holo holds exactly the 12 Legends; Epic champion units stay in Rare as in Origins.

## Grail and Ultra art briefs (hand-made, full art)

All eight are borderless full-art heroes with a "night jungle" look: deep greens and violets, mist, glowing eyes in the dark, moonlight. Each Signature carries a small unreadable gold signature scrawl in a lower corner; the Baron gets the animated rainbow-gold Grail frame and no signature.

- *Baron Nashor (Ultimate)* (Grail, Chaos): the great void serpent rears out of its pit in the middle of the jungle, many-eyed armoured head crowned with horn plates, two clawed tentacles gripping broken stone pillars, acid-green drool hissing on the rocks; tiny champion silhouettes at the edge of the pit for scale; purple sky cracked by green lightning. Deep purple, toxic green, bone, gold rim light.
- *Diana, Scorn of the Moon (Signature)* (Grail, Mind): Diana dives head-first out of the sky, crescent blade swept back, silver hair streaming; a huge full moon behind her forms a halo and crescent shards orbit her; far below, a dark jungle temple. Silver, pale blue, violet night, white moonlight.
- *LeBlanc, Deceiver (Signature)* (Ultra, Mind): LeBlanc steps out of a tall gilded mirror with a knowing smile behind her gold mask, while three translucent copies of her bow inside the reflection; violet smoke curls along the floor. Violet, gold, black, ruby accents.
- *Vex, Gloomist (Signature)* (Ultra, Calm): the tiny yordle slouches on a mossy rock with her hood up, deeply unimpressed; her shadow towers behind her as a huge grinning shape with glowing eyes swallowing a sunny jungle clearing. Dark purple, teal glow, sickly sunshine yellow.
- *Vi, Piltover Enforcer (Signature)* (Ultra, Fury): Vi punches straight at the viewer with one giant hextech gauntlet, the other cocked back, a brick wall bursting into fragments around her; badge on her coat, pink hair whipping. Pink, steel blue, hextech cyan.
- *Jhin, Virtuoso (Signature)* (Ultra, Fury): Jhin takes a deep theatrical bow at the edge of a stage, holding his ornate long rifle out like a conductor's baton; exactly four petals of fire fall around him, red curtains behind. Crimson, gold, white mask, stage-light yellow.
- *Pyke, Bloodharbor Ripper (Signature)* (Ultra, Fury): Pyke rises waist-deep from black harbour water, harpoon raised, kelp and chains hanging off him, teal eyes glowing; a wrecked ship and swinging lanterns behind. Teal, sea green, rust red, black.
- *Rengar, Pridestalker (Signature)* (Ultra, Fury): Rengar crouches on a giant monster skull at a cliff edge, one eye glowing, blade drawn, trophy necklace rattling; dusk jungle canopy below. Amber, deep green, bone white.

## Community lore & meme material

- **The Baron nobody pulls.** Riftbound's first "Ultimate" card: Baron Nashor (238/219), in under 0.1 % of packs and never more than one per pack (Riot). Raw about US$1,260–1,870 in completed sales, PSA 10 about US$2,600. The normal Epic Baron (147) sells for about US$20–25 and its alt art for about US$20: "you pulled Baron, just not that Baron".
- **Six Overnumbered Poros.** 220–225 are Overnumbered Poros (Pouty, Lonely, Plundering, Veteran, Mystic, Daring), mostly reprints of earlier commons; Lonely Poro is the priciest at about US$231–331, a lonely poro worth more than most booster boxes.
- **Diana beats LeBlanc.** Diana's Signature (US$1,311–1,530) is the most valuable Signature of the set; LeBlanc (about US$965–989) is a distant second.
- **"A teamfight gank".** Riot introduced the display box art with Vi, Rengar, Master Yi and LeBlanc as the champions who best embody a League teamfight gank.
- **The whole jungle in cardboard.** Smite is a common, Scuttle Crab a Rare, Gromp a common, plus the red and blue buff monsters, Rift Herald, Elder Dragon and Baron; the new mechanics are XP, Ambush and Hunt.
- **Two lotteries per set.** From Unleashed on, Riot's store holds two drawing windows per set, one before and one after launch, with expanded bot checks (Riot, April merch update).
- **Competitive surge.** The Atlanta Regional Qualifier drew more than 1,800 players the weekend before Unleashed launched (OpenCritic).
- **Bans.** Star Spring (UNL-215) was banned from 23 Nov 2026 together with Spiritforged's Emperor's Dais (Riot: too strong with Riven, no risk in conquering). Master Yi's old Proving Grounds Legend, Wuju Bladesman, was banned in 2v2 in July 2026 because his bonus also reached the teammate.
- **Jhin and the number four (LoL meme, not re-verified this pass).** Jhin's basic attack works in fours; every joke about him counts to four.
- **Master Yi (LoL meme, not re-verified this pass).** The low-elo nightmare who presses Alpha Strike and types "?" in chat.
- **Yuumi (LoL meme, not re-verified this pass).** The magical cat that sits on a teammate and is the most complained-about support.
- **Pyke (LoL meme, not re-verified this pass).** The support whose ultimate kills and shares gold, so "the support stole my kill" is literally his kit.
- **Rengar vs Kha'Zix (LoL lore, not re-verified this pass).** Rengar lost his eye to Kha'Zix and hunts him as the ultimate trophy; both are Unleashed Legends, but only Rengar made Ultra.
